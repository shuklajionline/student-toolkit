How Many Lectures Can I Bunk and Still Maintain 75% Attendance?

One of the most searched questions by college students is: “How many lectures can I bunk and still maintain 75% attendance?”

In most Indian colleges, the minimum attendance requirement is 75%. If your attendance falls below this limit, you may face exam restrictions or penalties.

Understanding the 75% Attendance Rule

Attendance percentage is calculated using this formula:

(Lectures Attended ÷ Total Lectures Conducted) × 100

To stay safe, your attendance percentage must remain above 75%.

Example Calculation

Suppose 100 lectures have been conducted. To maintain 75% attendance:

You must attend at least 75 lectures. That means you can bunk up to 25 lectures in total.

However, if you have already attended fewer lectures early in the semester, your safe bunk limit decreases.

Important Tips to Avoid Attendance Shortage

  • Maintain attendance above 80% for safety buffer.
  • Avoid consecutive bunk streaks.
  • Track attendance weekly instead of at semester end.
  • Be careful with practical and core subjects.

Final Advice

Bunking occasionally is normal. But smart students calculate their attendance before skipping classes.
