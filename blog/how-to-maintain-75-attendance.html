How to Maintain 75% Attendance in College

Maintaining 75% attendance is mandatory in many Indian colleges...

Attendance percentage is calculated as:
(Lectures Attended ÷ Total Lectures) × 100

To instantly calculate your attendance, use our Attendance Calculator.

Smart tips:

  • Keep attendance above 80% for safety buffer.
  • Track weekly instead of end of semester.
  • Avoid continuous bunk streaks.
  • Prioritize core subjects.
