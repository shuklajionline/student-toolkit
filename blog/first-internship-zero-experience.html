How to get your first internship in India with zero experience

Updated 9 October 2026 · 20 min read · Student Toolkit · Sources linked at the end

Short answer: pick one skill, ship one small public project, send 20–30 specific applications on Internshala in a week, and cold email 10 small startups. Freshers who do all four usually get their first reply in 2–4 weeks. The rest of this page is the exact plan, scripts included.

Every internship post says "experience preferred" and every fresher has none. The way out is not waiting for permission: companies hiring interns expect a student, so they screen for proof you can do a small piece of work and a clear, specific message. Both can be built in a few weeks.

These numbers come from Internshala's Annual Internship Trends Report 2024, the most recent full-year dataset we could verify. Treat them as direction, not promises.

Source: Internshala Annual Internship Trends Report 2024. Internships grew 25% over 2023. Delhi-NCR (31%), Mumbai (17%) and Bengaluru (11%) led on location.

What this means for you: most first internships are marketing, sales, content, operations or entry-level tech, and nearly half are remote. A student in Vadodara, Patna or Vijayawada competes for the same listings as one in Delhi.

Four myths that keep freshers stuck

"I need experience before I can apply."
The listing is the entry point. One small project counts as evidence.
"My CGPA is too low."
Most startups set no cutoff for interns. Your project and message decide.
"I need someone to refer me."
Open platforms and cold email work for strangers. Referrals help, they are not required.
"Apply to 3 places and wait."
Replies are rare. 20–30 applications a week is the normal pace for a first offer.

The 30-day plan

Week 1: build the proof

  • Pick the track above. Spend 3–4 days on the project, not on tutorials.
  • Publish it where a link works: GitHub, Behance, Google Drive folder or a free blog.
  • Write 3 lines on what it is, what you used and what you learned.

Week 2: set up your profiles

  • Make a one-page resume: skills, projects with links, education, certificates, clubs. No empty sections, no photo needed.
  • Complete your Internshala profile to 100% and add a LinkedIn photo and headline with your skill, such as "Frontend learner | React | open to internships".
  • Use the Letter & Email Writer to draft your first cover note, then edit it by hand.

Week 3: apply in volume

  • Apply to 25 listings that match your skill, with a unique 2-line answer to "why should we hire you".
  • Send 10 cold emails (script below). Log every application in a simple sheet: company, date, link, status.

Week 4: follow up and interview

  • Follow up once after 5–7 days, then move on.
  • Prepare 3 stories: your project, a time you learned something fast, a team task. Practise saying them out loud.
  • Repeat the application batch. Most first offers arrive on the second round.

The zero-experience resume that gets shortlisted

Recruiters spend seconds on a fresher resume. Make those seconds count with this order and nothing more than one page:

  1. Header: name, a professional email (not cooldude99@), phone, city, LinkedIn and portfolio/GitHub link.
  2. Projects (2-3): each with a link and a result.
  3. Skills: specific tools, such as "Python, Excel (pivot tables), Canva, SEO basics".
  4. Education: degree, college, year, CGPA only if it is decent.
  5. Certificates and activities: only relevant ones, plus club or event roles.

Write every bullet as what you built + tool + result. Weak: "Made a website for college fest." Strong: "Built a registration site for a 400-student fest using HTML/CSS and Google Forms; cut manual sign-up work by half." Use numbers you can defend in an interview. Save as PDF, name the file YourName-Role.pdf and keep one resume per track.

How many applications do you really need?

There is no official number, but our rule of thumb for an internship for freshers is a 5-10% reply rate. At that rate, 25 specific applications plus 10 cold emails usually means 2-4 conversations, so plan for 35+ outreach attempts before judging results. Generic mass applications reply far less, which is why the plan insists on one personal line per message.

What to expect to earn: the 2024 average was about ₹8,000 a month and the PM Internship Scheme reports ₹9,000, but many first internships pay less or are unpaid. Prefer a paid internship when offered, but a real unpaid one with a mentor and a letter beats a paid one that gives you no work.

Copy-paste scripts

Cold email to a founder

Subject: Internship enquiry | [Skill] | [College] Hi [First name], I'm [Name], a [year] year [branch] student at [college]. I liked [one specific thing: a feature, post or launch] at [Company]. I'm looking for a [duration] [role] internship and can start immediately. Here is a small [project] I built: [link]. I'm happy to do a short paid or unpaid trial task so you can judge my work. Thanks for reading, [Name] | [phone] | [LinkedIn]

Answer to "Why should we hire you?" on Internshala

Hi [Company], I built [project] using [tools] and can do [task they listed] from day one. I read your [blog/product] and noticed [one specific observation]. I can give [hours] a day for [duration]. Project link: [link].

Follow-up after 5–7 days

Hi [First name], following up on my note from last week about a [role] internship. I've attached my latest work: [link]. If the timing isn't right, no problem, and thank you for your time.

Never write "I am a student looking for an opportunity." Nobody reads past it.

Internship interview questions with sample answers

Tell me about yourself.

Two lines on your course and skill, one on your best project, one on why this role. Keep it under 60 seconds.

Why should we hire you with no experience?

I've built [project], I learn fast, and I can give [hours] a day. Offer a small trial task.

Walk me through your project.

Problem, what you built, one hard thing you fixed, the result. Know every line or choice you made.

What is your weakness?

Pick a real, fixable one, for example: I over-polish, so I now set a time limit per task.

What do you know about us?

Name one product, post or recent launch and one improvement idea. This alone beats most candidates.

Do you have questions for us?

Ask what a great first month looks like and who you will report to.

Seven mistakes that get freshers rejected

  1. One generic message sent to everyone.
  2. No link to any work.
  3. Skipping the screening questions on Internshala.
  4. Applying to 3 places, then waiting.
  5. A casual email ID or a resume over one page.
  6. No follow-up, or five follow-ups.
  7. Paying a "registration fee" to a fake internship.

LinkedIn headline and message to a recruiter

Headline: [Skill] learner | Built [project] | Open to internships (remote or [city]) Message: Hi [Name], I'm a [year] year [branch] student. I built [project] ([link]) and am looking for a [role] internship. Could I send you my resume for any opening on your team? Thank you.

Work-from-home and online internships: real or fake?

A work from home internship or virtual internship is real when you get a named manager, defined tasks, weekly check-ins and a signed certificate. Roughly half of listings were remote in 2024, so you can apply beyond your city, including as a first year student. An online internship with certificate that charges you money, or promises a certificate without work, is a scam.

Low CGPA, backlog or tier-2/3 college?

Put projects above marks and leave CGPA off if it is weak. Say honestly "learning X, built Y". Remote roles, startups and cold email do not filter by college tier, so they are your best bet. Plan the summer internship 2026 and winter hunts early: start applying 6-8 weeks before your break.

Scam red flags

  • Any fee for registration, a "security deposit", a certificate or training kit. Real internships do not charge you.
  • Offers on WhatsApp or Telegram only, with no company email, website or named manager.
  • "Selected" within minutes of applying, with no interview or task.
  • A request for Aadhaar, bank details or an OTP before any offer letter.
Search the company name plus "scam" and check that its LinkedIn page has real employees before you reply.

After you get in: turn it into a PPO or a strong reference

  • Week 1: ask what a good first month looks like, then write it down.
  • Every Friday: send your manager a 3-line update of what you finished.
  • Last week: ask for a letter of recommendation and a LinkedIn recommendation, and ask whether a PPO or extension is possible.
  • If it pays, use the Salary Calculator later to compare full-time offers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an internship in India with no experience?

Pick one skill, build one small public project, make a one-page resume led by projects, apply to 20–30 listings with a specific answer to "why should we hire you", and cold email 10 small startups.

Which is the best platform for fresher internships?

Internshala is the best starting point for most students. Add LinkedIn, Unstop, company career pages and cold email.

What is the average internship stipend in India?

Internshala's 2024 report put it at about ₹8,000 a month, with top offers up to ₹1,00,000. Many first internships pay less or nothing.

Can I get an internship with a low CGPA?

Yes. Most startups and small companies set no CGPA cutoff for interns. A relevant project and a clear application matter more.

How long does it take to get a first internship?

With 20+ applications a week plus cold emails, many students get a first reply in 2–4 weeks. Follow up once, then keep applying.

What should a fresher write on a resume with no experience?

Lead with 2-3 linked projects, then skills, education, certificates and club roles. Keep it to one page and write bullets as what you built, the tool and the result.

What are common internship interview questions for freshers?

Tell me about yourself, why this role, your project, your weakness, your availability and what you know about the company. See the sample answers above.

Is an internship compulsory in college in India?

Many universities now include a credit internship, but rules differ by course. Check your syllabus or ask your department.

Can first year students get internships?

Yes. Short remote internships, skill-building roles and competitions are open to first year students.

Are work from home internships real?

Many are, if you get a named manager, defined tasks and a free certificate. Any internship that asks for a fee is a scam.

How many internships should I apply to?

Plan 35 or more outreach attempts (25 applications and 10 cold emails) before judging results, and personalise each one.

Can ChatGPT or Claude teach me a skill for an internship?

Yes, as a structured tutor. Ask for a 14-day course with theory, examples, exercises, quizzes and a project, one day at a time, and check facts and code against official documentation.

Should I take an unpaid internship?

Only if you get real work, a named mentor and a certificate or letter. Never pay a fee to get an internship.

Sources: Internshala Annual Internship Trends Report 2024, Internshala PM Internship guide. Always confirm current rules on pminternship.mca.gov.in.
