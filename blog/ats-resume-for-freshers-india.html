How to Make an ATS-Friendly Resume as a Fresher in India (2027)

The exact one-page format, section-by-section examples for every stream, copy-paste templates and a 20-point self-check — so your resume gets read by a human, not rejected by software.

The short version

  • Use one page, one column, standard headings and a text-based PDF. Fancy templates with columns, icons and skill bars often break ATS parsing.
  • Order for freshers: Contact → Summary (optional) → Education → Skills → Projects → Internships/Experience → Achievements → Positions of responsibility.
  • Write every bullet as action verb + what you did + measurable result. “Built a Flask app used by 120 classmates” beats “Worked on a web project”.
  • Tailor 6–10 keywords from each job description into your skills and bullets — honestly. Keyword-stuffing gets caught by the human reading next.
  • Leave out photo, date of birth, father’s name, religion, marital status, “Declaration” and a list of hobbies with no evidence.
  • Score yourself with the 20-point checklist before every application.

What an ATS actually does (and why freshers get filtered out)

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is the software companies use to collect and sort applications — Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit and the systems behind Naukri, LinkedIn and Internshala. When you upload a resume, the ATS parses it: it tries to pull out your name, contact details, education, dates, skills and job titles into fields a recruiter can search and filter.

Two things go wrong for freshers:

  1. Parsing fails. Two-column Canva templates, text inside images, tables, headers/footers and icons can scramble the order or hide text completely. The recruiter then sees a half-empty profile.
  2. Filters and searches miss you. Recruiters search for terms like “Python”, “Excel”, “SQL”, “B.Com” or “2027”, and filter by graduation year, CGPA or location. If those words aren’t in your resume in plain text, you don’t appear.
Myth check

There is no universal “ATS score” that every company uses. Online “ATS scores” are rough estimates. What matters is: can software read your resume cleanly, and do the words a recruiter searches for appear in it? This guide fixes both.

After the ATS, a human — often a recruiter screening hundreds of resumes for a campus or internship drive — skims your page in well under a minute. So the resume has to work twice: machine-readable and instantly convincing to a person.

Quick test

Open your PDF, press Ctrl + A and copy everything into Notepad. If the text comes out in the right order with nothing missing, most ATS will read it. If columns are jumbled or text is missing, change the layout.

Skill bars are the worst offender. “Python ●●●●○” tells a recruiter nothing and an ATS reads nothing. Replace them with evidence: “Python — built 3 projects incl. a stock-price dashboard (Pandas, Plotly)”.

The right sections, in the right order

For a fresher, recruiters care about proof of ability more than job titles. Put your strongest evidence high on the page.

  1. Header / contact. Full name, phone with +91, a professional email (firstname.lastname@…), city, LinkedIn URL, and GitHub/portfolio/Behance if relevant. No full address, no photo.
  2. Summary (optional, 2 lines). Only if it’s specific: “Final-year B.Tech (CSE) student with 2 full-stack projects and a 3-month backend internship, seeking SDE roles.” Skip generic “hard-working, passionate” lines.
  3. Education. Degree, college, university, years, CGPA/percentage. Add Class 12 and 10 with board and percentage (Indian recruiters still check these). Relevant coursework in one line if it adds keywords.
  4. Skills. Grouped: Languages, Tools, Frameworks, Domain. Only what you can be interviewed on.
  5. Projects. 2–4 projects, each with 2–3 bullets and a link. For most freshers this is the most important section.
  6. Internships / experience. Including virtual internships, freelance work, family business, teaching, campus jobs — anything real.
  7. Achievements & certifications. Ranks, hackathons, case competitions, scholarships, published work. Certificates only if they’re recognised (NPTEL with score, AWS/Google/Microsoft, CFA level, NISM) — not 15 random course completions.
  8. Positions of responsibility / extracurriculars. Club roles, fest organising, sports at a competitive level, NSS/NCC — with numbers.
Should I write CGPA?

Yes, if the job or campus drive asks for it — many Indian companies filter on CGPA or percentage. If it’s low and not required, you can leave it out, but expect to be asked in the interview. How to write bullets that get shortlisted

Use the formula Action verb + what you did (with which tool) + result (number). Numbers can be users, time saved, accuracy, money, rank, size of dataset, number of people managed.

Weak

  • Worked on a machine learning project.
  • Responsible for social media of college fest.
  • Did an internship at a CA firm.
  • Good communication skills.

Strong

  • Trained a Random Forest model on 25,000 loan records to predict defaults with 87% accuracy (Python, scikit-learn).
  • Grew fest Instagram from 1.2k to 6.8k followers in 6 weeks with a 30-post content calendar; 3 reels crossed 50k views.
  • Prepared GST returns for 18 clients and reconciled ₹40 lakh of invoices in Tally during a 2-month articleship.
  • Presented a 15-minute product pitch to a 4-member jury; team placed 2nd of 64 at the B-school case challenge.

Action verbs that work

Build/tech: built, developed, automated, deployed, optimised, debugged, integrated, designed. Analysis: analysed, forecast, modelled, reconciled, audited, researched, benchmarked. People/business: led, organised, negotiated, pitched, coordinated, trained, sold, grew. Avoid “responsible for”, “worked on”, “helped with”.

No numbers? Find them

  • How many people used it, attended, or were in the team?
  • How long did it take before vs after your change?
  • How big was the data, budget, audience or inventory?
  • What rank, grade, or selection rate (e.g., “top 5 of 300 applicants”)?

No internship yet? What to put instead

Most freshers worry about the empty “Experience” section. Recruiters hiring freshers know you don’t have years of work — they look for initiative and proof. Fill the page with things you can start this week:

Tailoring keywords for each job (without stuffing)

  1. Copy the job description into a document and highlight skills, tools and qualifications (e.g., “SQL”, “stakeholder communication”, “Power BI”, “B.Tech 2027”).
  2. Mark the ones you genuinely have. Never add skills you can’t explain in an interview.
  3. Mirror the exact wording. If the JD says “Microsoft Excel” and you wrote “MS-Excel”, use the JD’s phrase once. Write both acronym and full form once: “Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)”.
  4. Place them where they carry proof — inside project and internship bullets, not only in the skills list.
  5. Keep a master resume with everything, and save a tailored copy per role type (SDE, data analyst, marketing, etc.).
Don’t do this

White text with hidden keywords, copying the whole JD into the resume, or listing 40 skills. Modern ATS and recruiters spot these, and they can get you blacklisted from a company’s portal.

12 mistakes Indian freshers make

  1. Adding a photo, date of birth, gender, religion, father’s name, marital status or nationality — not needed for most private-sector jobs in India.
  2. A “Declaration: I hereby declare…” line at the bottom. It wastes space.
  3. An objective like “To work in a reputed organisation where I can utilise my skills.” Say what role you want or skip it.
  4. Listing MS Word, MS PowerPoint and “Internet browsing” as skills.
  5. Hobbies like “listening to music, travelling”. Keep a hobby only if it shows something (state-level chess, running a 3k-subscriber channel).
  6. Fifteen online certificates from free course sites with no projects.
  7. Spelling mistakes in the college name, company name or your own email.
  8. Unprofessional email IDs (cool.boy.007@…).
  9. Inconsistent date formats and missing graduation year — recruiters filter by batch.
  10. Broken links to GitHub or portfolio. Open every link before sending.
  11. Two pages for a fresher, or 8-pt font to fit one.
  12. Lying about internships or certifications. Background verification firms check, and offers get revoked.

Where and how to send it

  • Campus placements: follow the placement cell format exactly — many colleges mandate a template. Keep your own one-page version for off-campus.
  • Naukri, LinkedIn, Internshala, Foundit, Wellfound: fill the profile fields fully; recruiters search those fields, not just the attached file. Update your profile weekly — recency affects visibility on job portals.
  • Company career pages: upload the tailored PDF and check the auto-filled fields after parsing; fix any mistakes manually.
  • Cold emails and referrals: a short, specific email to an alumnus or hiring manager often works better than 100 portal applications. Draft one in seconds with our letter & email writer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best resume format for freshers in India?

A one-page, single-column reverse-chronological resume with standard headings: contact details, short summary (optional), education with CGPA, skills, projects, internships, achievements and positions of responsibility. Save it as a text-based PDF.

Should I put a photo on my resume in India?

For most private-sector, tech, consulting and finance jobs, no. A photo takes space, can confuse ATS parsing and is not needed. Add one only if the employer or campus template explicitly asks for it.

Is a Canva resume ATS-friendly?

Many Canva templates use columns, icons and text boxes that ATS software parses badly. If you use Canva, choose a single-column template with no graphics and test it by copying the PDF text into Notepad.

How long should a fresher resume be?

One page. Recruiters screening freshers spend very little time per resume, and a focused page with strong projects beats two pages of filler.

How do I write a resume with no work experience?

Lead with education, projects and skills. Add academic, personal or freelance projects with links and numbers, virtual internships, competitions, campus roles and volunteering. Every bullet should show what you did and the result.

Should I mention CGPA if it is low?

If the job or campus drive asks for CGPA or sets a cutoff, you must state it honestly. If it is not required, you can leave it out and let projects and skills carry the resume, but be ready to answer about it in interviews.

PDF or Word — which is better for ATS?

A text-based PDF works with most modern ATS and keeps formatting intact. Use .docx only when the portal asks for it. Never upload a scanned image PDF.
