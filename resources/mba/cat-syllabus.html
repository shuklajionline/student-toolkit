CAT Syllabus 2026 – Complete Section Wise Breakdown

The Common Admission Test (CAT) is the gateway to IIMs and top MBA colleges in India. Understanding the CAT syllabus 2026 is essential for structured preparation.

1. VARC (Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension)

  • Reading Comprehension
  • Para Jumbles
  • Summary Writing
  • Odd One Out

2. DILR (Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning)

  • Tables & Graphs
  • Seating Arrangement
  • Logical Puzzles
  • Caselets

3. Quantitative Aptitude

  • Arithmetic
  • Algebra
  • Geometry
  • Number System
  • Modern Maths

CAT preparation requires consistency and sectional balance. Refer to the official CAT portal for updates.
