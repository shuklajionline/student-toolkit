UPSC Civil Services Syllabus 2026 – Complete Breakdown

The UPSC Civil Services Examination syllabus is divided into Prelims and Mains stages. Understanding the syllabus is the first step toward structured preparation.

UPSC Prelims Syllabus

General Studies Paper I

  • Current events of national and international importance
  • History of India and Indian National Movement
  • Indian and World Geography
  • Indian Polity and Governance
  • Economic and Social Development
  • Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity and Climate Change
  • General Science

CSAT (Paper II)

  • Comprehension
  • Logical Reasoning and Analytical Ability
  • Basic Numeracy
  • Decision Making

UPSC Mains Syllabus

Essay Paper

Candidates write essays on multiple topics to test analytical ability.

General Studies Papers

  • GS I – Indian Heritage and Culture, History and Geography
  • GS II – Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice
  • GS III – Economy, Science & Technology, Environment, Security
  • GS IV – Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude

Optional Subject

Candidates choose one optional subject carrying 500 marks.
