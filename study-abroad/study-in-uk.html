Study in UK for Indian Students 2027

1-year master’s, Graduate Route work visa, strong global brand.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

UK student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student visa (CAS from university). £558 visa fee (from 8 April 2026) + Immigration Health Surcharge, charged per year of visa (widely reported as £776/year for students — confirm on GOV.UK).

About 3 weeks from biometrics outside the UK; priority services available.

Money you must show

Course fees for year one (minus any deposit paid) plus living funds: £1,483/month in London or £1,136/month outside London, for up to 9 months, held for 28 consecutive days.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your CAS.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 20 hrs/week in term for degree-level study; full-time in official vacations.

Post-study work

Graduate Route: 2 years if you apply on or before 31 Dec 2026; 18 months for applications from 1 Jan 2027. PhD graduates get 3 years. Graduate visa fee £937.

Permanent residence

Possible Switch to a Skilled Worker visa (salary thresholds were raised in 2025) to stay long-term. Government has proposed lengthening the route to settlement to 10 years.

Language for jobs

English.

Things Indian students must know

  • Taught master’s students can no longer bring dependants (since Jan 2024) — only research degrees qualify.
  • Your money must sit in the account for 28 consecutive days before you apply.
  • From 1 Jan 2027 the Graduate Route drops to 18 months — plan your course end date accordingly.
  • Intakes: September — main intake, apply Oct–Jun (UCAS deadline late Jan for UG); January — many PG programs; May — limited programs.

Pros

  • 1-year master’s saves a year of fees and living costs
  • Graduate Route lets you work without a job offer
  • IELTS waivers common for strong Class 12 English
  • World-famous universities and short flights from India

Cons

  • Graduate Route shortened to 18 months from 2027
  • High London living costs
  • Skilled Worker salary thresholds are high
  • No dependants on taught master’s

FAQs: studying in UK

How much does it cost to study in UK for Indian students?

A master’s in UK costs about ₹35.1 lakh – ₹71.6 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹35.1 lakh–₹71.6 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹33.9 lakh – ₹69.2 lakh a year. Most taught master’s last 12 months, so total cost is often lower than a 2-year degree elsewhere. MBA £35k–90k+. Medicine/clinical degrees cost more.

How much money do I need to show for a UK student visa?

Course fees for year one (minus any deposit paid) plus living funds: £1,483/month in London or £1,136/month outside London, for up to 9 months, held for 28 consecutive days.

Can Indian students work part-time in UK?

Up to 20 hrs/week in term for degree-level study; full-time in official vacations.

How long can I stay in UK after graduation?

Graduate Route: 2 years if you apply on or before 31 Dec 2026; 18 months for applications from 1 Jan 2027. PhD graduates get 3 years. Graduate visa fee £937.

Can I get PR in UK after studying?

Possible outlook. Switch to a Skilled Worker visa (salary thresholds were raised in 2025) to stay long-term. Government has proposed lengthening the route to settlement to 10 years.

Do I need IELTS to study in UK?

IELTS for UKVI or university-accepted tests. Many universities waive IELTS if Class 12 English is 70–80%+ (CBSE/ISC) — check each university.

What are the intakes in UK?

September — main intake, apply Oct–Jun (UCAS deadline late Jan for UG). January — many PG programs. May — limited programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in UK?

Chevening Scholarships (PG): Fully funded 1-year master’s, needs 2 years’ work experience. Commonwealth Master’s Scholarships (PG): Fully funded, for development-related subjects. GREAT Scholarships (PG): £10,000 at participating universities. University merit awards (UG/PG): Typically £2,000–£10,000 off tuition.
