Study in USA for Indian Students 2027

Top-ranked universities, STEM OPT up to 3 years, highest costs.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

USA student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

F-1 student visa (I-20 from university). $185 visa (MRV) fee + $350 SEVIS I-901 fee. A $250 “visa integrity fee” was legislated in 2025 — check travel.state.gov for whether it is being collected at your post.

Interview wait times vary by consulate (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata); book as soon as you get your I-20.

Money you must show

No fixed government figure. Show one year of tuition + living as stated on your I-20 (bank statements, loan sanction letters, sponsor affidavits).

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your I-20 (then pay the SEVIS fee).
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics; attend the interview.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 20 hrs/week on-campus during term; full-time in breaks. Off-campus work only via CPT/OPT.

Post-study work

OPT: 12 months after graduation. STEM degrees get a 24-month STEM OPT extension (36 months total).

Permanent residence

Possible Long-term stay usually means H-1B (lottery) then a green card. Employment green-card backlogs for Indian nationals are very long. A 2025 proclamation added a $100,000 fee on certain new H-1B petitions — USCIS has said change of status from F-1 inside the US is not covered; check the latest guidance.

Language for jobs

English.

Things Indian students must know

  • Social-media identifiers are reviewed during visa screening — keep public profiles consistent with your application.
  • Choose a STEM-designated program (CIP code on the I-20) if you want the 24-month STEM OPT extension.
  • Out-of-state tuition rises 3–5% a year; budget for it.
  • Intakes: Fall (Aug–Sep) — main intake, apply Oct–Feb; Spring (Jan) — fewer programs, apply Jul–Oct.

Pros

  • Widest choice of top universities and programs
  • STEM OPT gives up to 3 years of US work experience
  • Highest graduate salaries in tech and finance
  • Assistantships can cut costs sharply

Cons

  • Most expensive destination overall
  • H-1B is a lottery; green-card backlog for Indians
  • Visa appointment waits and stricter screening
  • Health insurance and living costs are high

FAQs: studying in USA

How much does it cost to study in USA for Indian students?

A master’s in USA costs about ₹29.9 lakh – ₹81.0 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹59.8 lakh–₹1.62 crore). A bachelor’s costs about ₹34.3 lakh – ₹85.4 lakh a year. Public universities charge out-of-state rates to international students ($25k–45k). Private universities typically $45k–65k. A 2-year MS usually costs $40k–100k in total tuition; MBA $120k–170k.

How much money do I need to show for a USA student visa?

No fixed government figure. Show one year of tuition + living as stated on your I-20 (bank statements, loan sanction letters, sponsor affidavits).

Can Indian students work part-time in USA?

Up to 20 hrs/week on-campus during term; full-time in breaks. Off-campus work only via CPT/OPT.

How long can I stay in USA after graduation?

OPT: 12 months after graduation. STEM degrees get a 24-month STEM OPT extension (36 months total).

Can I get PR in USA after studying?

Possible outlook. Long-term stay usually means H-1B (lottery) then a green card. Employment green-card backlogs for Indian nationals are very long. A 2025 proclamation added a $100,000 fee on certain new H-1B petitions — USCIS has said change of status from F-1 inside the US is not covered; check the latest guidance.

Do I need IELTS to study in USA?

IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 80–100 typical. Duolingo widely accepted. GRE optional at many MS programs; GMAT/GRE for MBA.

What are the intakes in USA?

Fall (Aug–Sep) — main intake, apply Oct–Feb. Spring (Jan) — fewer programs, apply Jul–Oct.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in USA?

Fulbright-Nehru Master’s Fellowships (PG): Full funding for selected Indian students (USIEF). University assistantships (TA/RA) (PG/PhD): Tuition waiver + stipend, common in PhD and some MS. Merit scholarships at public universities (UG/PG): Partial tuition reductions. Tata Scholarship (Cornell) (UG): Need-based, for Indian students at Cornell.
