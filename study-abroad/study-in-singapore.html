Study in Singapore for Indian Students 2027

Asia’s top universities 4 hours from India — no post-study work visa.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Singapore costs an Indian student about ₹31.2 lakh – ₹65.8 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹31.2 lakh – ₹59.0 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 16 hours a week while studying and stay none after graduation to work.

Singapore student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student’s Pass (ICA, via SOLAR). About S$120 total processing and issuance fees.

About 2–4 weeks.

Money you must show

No fixed figure; universities assess finances; MOE Tuition Grant holders sign a bond.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 16 hrs/week in term for full-time students at approved institutions; full-time in vacations.

Post-study work

No post-study work visa. You need a job offer for an Employment Pass (scored under COMPASS) or S Pass. A 1-year Long-Term Visit Pass may be available to search.

Permanent residence

Possible PR possible after working on an Employment Pass, but approval is selective.

Language for jobs

English.

FAQs: studying in Singapore

How much does it cost to study in Singapore for Indian students?

A master’s in Singapore costs about ₹31.2 lakh – ₹65.8 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹31.2 lakh–₹65.8 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹31.2 lakh – ₹59.0 lakh a year. NUS/NTU/SMU: the MOE Tuition Grant lowers fees but requires a 3-year bond to work in Singapore. Master’s programs are usually not subsidised.

How much money do I need to show for a Singapore student visa?

No fixed figure; universities assess finances; MOE Tuition Grant holders sign a bond.

Can Indian students work part-time in Singapore?

Up to 16 hrs/week in term for full-time students at approved institutions; full-time in vacations.

How long can I stay in Singapore after graduation?

No post-study work visa. You need a job offer for an Employment Pass (scored under COMPASS) or S Pass. A 1-year Long-Term Visit Pass may be available to search.

Can I get PR in Singapore after studying?

Possible outlook. PR possible after working on an Employment Pass, but approval is selective.

Do I need IELTS to study in Singapore?

IELTS 6.5 typical; CBSE/ISC English may be accepted for UG.

What are the intakes in Singapore?

August — main. January — some master’s.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Singapore?

Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA) (PhD): Full tuition + monthly stipend. MOE Tuition Grant (UG): Lower fees with a 3-year work bond. ASEAN/University scholarships (UG/PG): Varies.
