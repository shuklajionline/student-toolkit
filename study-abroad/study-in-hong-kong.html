Study in Hong Kong for Indian Students 2027

Top-ranked universities and a 2-year IANG post-study visa.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Hong Kong costs an Indian student about ₹28.0 lakh – ₹64.4 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹28.0 lakh – ₹47.5 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 2 years after graduation to work.

Hong Kong student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student visa/entry permit (sponsored by university). About HK$230.

About 4–6 weeks.

Money you must show

Show funds for tuition and living for the study period.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 20 hrs/week on campus; summer jobs allowed. A temporary relaxation lets some taught-postgrad students work part-time — check Immigration Department.

Post-study work

Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG): 24 months to stay and work.

Permanent residence

Moderate Permanent residency after 7 years of continuous ordinary residence.

Language for jobs

English and Cantonese.

FAQs: studying in Hong Kong

How much does it cost to study in Hong Kong for Indian students?

A master’s in Hong Kong costs about ₹28.0 lakh – ₹64.4 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹28.0 lakh–₹64.4 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹28.0 lakh – ₹47.5 lakh a year. Taught master’s are usually 1 year. Business/finance programs at the top end.

How much money do I need to show for a Hong Kong student visa?

Show funds for tuition and living for the study period.

Can Indian students work part-time in Hong Kong?

Up to 20 hrs/week on campus; summer jobs allowed. A temporary relaxation lets some taught-postgrad students work part-time — check Immigration Department.

How long can I stay in Hong Kong after graduation?

Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG): 24 months to stay and work.

Can I get PR in Hong Kong after studying?

Moderate outlook. Permanent residency after 7 years of continuous ordinary residence.

Do I need IELTS to study in Hong Kong?

IELTS 6.5 typical.

What are the intakes in Hong Kong?

September — main.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Hong Kong?

Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (PhD): Monthly stipend + travel allowance. University entrance scholarships (UG/PG): Partial to full tuition.
