Study in Ireland for Indian Students 2027

English-speaking EU country with big tech and pharma employers.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Ireland costs an Indian student about ₹25.2 lakh – ₹51.9 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹24.2 lakh – ₹46.8 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 2 years after graduation to work.

Ireland student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Study (D) visa + IRP registration on arrival. €60 single-entry visa; €300 IRP registration on arrival (confirm current fees with Irish Immigration).

About 4–8 weeks.

Money you must show

Show at least €10,000 per year of immediately accessible funds, plus your tuition fees.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Stamp 2: 20 hrs/week in term; 40 hrs/week in June–September and 15 Dec–15 Jan.

Post-study work

Stamp 1G Graduate scheme: 12 months after a bachelor’s (level 8), up to 24 months after a master’s (level 9).

Permanent residence

Moderate Critical Skills Employment Permit can lead to Stamp 4 after 2 years; general employment permits take longer.

Language for jobs

English.

Things Indian students must know

  • European HQs of Google, Meta, Apple, Pfizer and others are in Ireland — strong for tech and pharma careers.
  • Housing shortage: arrange accommodation before you fly.
  • Intakes: September — main intake; January/February — limited programs.

Pros

  • English-speaking EU country
  • Up to 2 years post-study work after a master’s
  • Tech and pharma employers based here
  • 1-year master’s

Cons

  • Severe housing shortage and high rents
  • Small job market compared with UK/US
  • Rainy climate

FAQs: studying in Ireland

How much does it cost to study in Ireland for Indian students?

A master’s in Ireland costs about ₹25.2 lakh – ₹51.9 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹25.2 lakh–₹51.9 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹24.2 lakh – ₹46.8 lakh a year. Most taught master’s are 1 year. Business and medicine programs cost more.

How much money do I need to show for a Ireland student visa?

Show at least €10,000 per year of immediately accessible funds, plus your tuition fees.

Can Indian students work part-time in Ireland?

Stamp 2: 20 hrs/week in term; 40 hrs/week in June–September and 15 Dec–15 Jan.

How long can I stay in Ireland after graduation?

Stamp 1G Graduate scheme: 12 months after a bachelor’s (level 8), up to 24 months after a master’s (level 9).

Can I get PR in Ireland after studying?

Moderate outlook. Critical Skills Employment Permit can lead to Stamp 4 after 2 years; general employment permits take longer.

Do I need IELTS to study in Ireland?

IELTS 6.5 typical; some universities accept Class 12 English scores.

What are the intakes in Ireland?

September — main intake. January/February — limited programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Ireland?

Government of Ireland International Education Scholarships (UG/PG): €10,000 stipend + full fee waiver for one year. University merit scholarships (UG/PG): €2,000–€5,000 off tuition is common.
