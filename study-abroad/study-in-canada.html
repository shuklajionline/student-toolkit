Study in Canada for Indian Students 2027

PGWP up to 3 years and established PR routes — now with study-permit caps.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Canada student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Study permit (+ PAL/TAL attestation letter). C$150 study permit + C$85 biometrics.

Varies; apply as soon as you have your letter of acceptance and PAL.

Money you must show

C$23,448 for a single applicant for living costs (effective 1 Sep 2026), plus first-year tuition and travel. Quebec sets its own, higher amount.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your letter of acceptance and PAL.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 24 hrs/week off-campus in term; full-time in scheduled breaks. Separate co-op work permit no longer needed for eligible post-secondary students (from April 2026).

Post-study work

Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) up to 3 years. Master’s of 1 year+ can get 3 years. College programs must be in an eligible field of study; language test (CLB 7 university / CLB 5 college) required.

Permanent residence

Strong Canadian work experience helps in Express Entry (Canadian Experience Class) and provincial nominee programs — still one of the clearest study-to-PR paths, but draws are more competitive than before.

Language for jobs

English (French in Quebec and some programs).

Things Indian students must know

  • Study permits are capped nationally (about 408,000 for 2026); most applicants need a provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL).
  • The Student Direct Stream (SDS) ended in November 2024 — everyone uses the regular stream.
  • Master’s and PhD students still qualify for open work permits for spouses; most others do not.
  • Intakes: September (Fall) — main, apply Oct–Mar; January (Winter) — apply Jun–Sep; May (Summer) — limited.

Pros

  • PGWP up to 3 years
  • Clearest PR pathway among popular destinations
  • Large Indian community and support networks
  • Work up to 24 hrs/week while studying

Cons

  • Study-permit caps and PAL requirement
  • Housing crisis in Toronto/Vancouver
  • Proof of funds rose to C$23,448
  • PGWP field-of-study rules for college programs

FAQs: studying in Canada

How much does it cost to study in Canada for Indian students?

A master’s in Canada costs about ₹24.5 lakh – ₹51.0 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹49.0 lakh–₹1.02 crore). A bachelor’s costs about ₹27.7 lakh – ₹54.1 lakh a year. Public colleges (diplomas/PG certificates) C$15k–22k/year. Universities C$25k–55k; Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal programs are at the top end.

How much money do I need to show for a Canada student visa?

C$23,448 for a single applicant for living costs (effective 1 Sep 2026), plus first-year tuition and travel. Quebec sets its own, higher amount.

Can Indian students work part-time in Canada?

Up to 24 hrs/week off-campus in term; full-time in scheduled breaks. Separate co-op work permit no longer needed for eligible post-secondary students (from April 2026).

How long can I stay in Canada after graduation?

Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) up to 3 years. Master’s of 1 year+ can get 3 years. College programs must be in an eligible field of study; language test (CLB 7 university / CLB 5 college) required.

Can I get PR in Canada after studying?

Strong outlook. Canadian work experience helps in Express Entry (Canadian Experience Class) and provincial nominee programs — still one of the clearest study-to-PR paths, but draws are more competitive than before.

Do I need IELTS to study in Canada?

IELTS Academic 6.5 typical for universities; PTE, TOEFL, CAEL and Duolingo accepted by many institutions.

What are the intakes in Canada?

September (Fall) — main, apply Oct–Mar. January (Winter) — apply Jun–Sep. May (Summer) — limited.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Canada?

Lester B. Pearson Scholarship (U of T) (UG): Full tuition, books and residence for 4 years. Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships (PhD): C$50,000/year for 3 years. University entrance scholarships (UG): C$1,000–C$20,000. Ontario Graduate Scholarship (PG): C$10,000–15,000 (limited for international students).
