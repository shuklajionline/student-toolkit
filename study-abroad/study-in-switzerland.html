Study in Switzerland for Indian Students 2027

World-class ETH/EPFL at modest fees — but the highest living costs.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Switzerland costs an Indian student about ₹26.7 lakh – ₹44.4 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹26.7 lakh – ₹44.4 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 15 hours a week while studying and stay 6 months after graduation to work.

Switzerland student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

National visa (D) + residence permit B. About CHF 88 + cantonal permit fees.

8–12 weeks.

Money you must show

About CHF 21,000+ per year, depending on the canton.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 15 hrs/week in term (after 6 months of residence).

Post-study work

6 months to look for a job after graduation.

Permanent residence

Limited Non-EU work permits are quota-based and hard to get.

Language for jobs

German, French or Italian by region; many master’s in English.

FAQs: studying in Switzerland

How much does it cost to study in Switzerland for Indian students?

A master’s in Switzerland costs about ₹26.7 lakh – ₹44.4 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹53.5 lakh–₹88.9 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹26.7 lakh – ₹44.4 lakh a year. Public universities charge CHF 1,500–8,000 per year; ETH Zurich raised fees for international students from autumn 2025. Hotel schools and private institutions cost far more.

How much money do I need to show for a Switzerland student visa?

About CHF 21,000+ per year, depending on the canton.

Can Indian students work part-time in Switzerland?

Up to 15 hrs/week in term (after 6 months of residence).

How long can I stay in Switzerland after graduation?

6 months to look for a job after graduation.

Can I get PR in Switzerland after studying?

Limited outlook. Non-EU work permits are quota-based and hard to get.

Do I need IELTS to study in Switzerland?

IELTS 7.0 for many ETH/EPFL master’s.

What are the intakes in Switzerland?

September — main (applications Nov–Apr).

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Switzerland?

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships (PhD/Postdoc): Research scholarships for foreign scholars. ETH Excellence Scholarship (PG): Living + tuition for top master’s students.
