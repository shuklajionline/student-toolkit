MBBS in Georgia for Indian Students 2027

English-medium MD programs in a safe, Europe-facing country.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: MBBS in Georgia costs an Indian student about ₹7.3 lakh – ₹12.0 lakh per year (tuition + living), or ₹43.8 lakh–₹71.8 lakh for the full 6-year program. You need NEET-UG, and the program must meet NMC’s foreign medical graduate rules to practise in India.

NMC checklist before you pay any fee

  • ✅ You have qualified NEET-UG (mandatory for MBBS abroad).
  • ✅ Course duration is at least 54 months (4.5 years) of study.
  • ✅ Entire course is taught in English.
  • ✅ 12-month internship is done at the same foreign university/hospital.
  • ✅ Graduates are eligible for a licence to practise medicine in Georgia.
  • ✅ You plan for FMGE/NExT and the Indian internship after returning.

FAQs: MBBS in Georgia

What is the total cost of MBBS in Georgia for Indian students?

Tuition is about $4,500–$8,000 a year and living about $300–$450 a month, so the full 6-year program costs roughly ₹43.8 lakh–₹71.8 lakh including living and insurance, before flights and hostel deposits.

Is MBBS from Georgia valid in India?

Only if the specific university and program meet the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021: at least 54 months of study, English medium, a 12-month internship at the same institution, and eligibility for a licence to practise in Georgia. You must then pass FMGE/NExT in India. Check the university’s compliance before paying any fee.

Is NEET required for MBBS in Georgia?

Yes. Indian students must qualify NEET-UG to study medicine abroad and later practise in India.

Do I need IELTS for MBBS in Georgia?

NEET-UG qualification required; IELTS not usually needed. Georgian language taught for patient interaction.

Which are the best medical universities in Georgia for Indian students?

Well-known options include Tbilisi State Medical University, Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University, Batumi Shota Rustaveli State University, David Tvildiani Medical University. Compare clinical exposure, FMGE pass rates of past Indian graduates, and NMC compliance before deciding.
