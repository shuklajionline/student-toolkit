Study in Japan for Indian Students 2027

Low national-university fees, MEXT scholarships and a tech job market short of talent.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Japan costs an Indian student about ₹9.5 lakh – ₹19.3 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹9.5 lakh – ₹19.3 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 28 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Japan student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student visa (with Certificate of Eligibility). Visa fee for Indian nationals is low; check the Embassy of Japan in India.

COE 1–3 months + visa about a week.

Money you must show

Show funds for tuition + living (often about ¥2–2.5 million for year one) to get a Certificate of Eligibility.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 28 hrs/week with a “permission to engage in activity”; 40 hrs/week in long vacations.

Post-study work

Change to “Designated Activities” for up to 1 year of job hunting.

Permanent residence

Possible Highly Skilled Professional points can lead to PR in 1–3 years.

Language for jobs

Japanese needed for most jobs and daily life.

Things Indian students must know

  • MEXT applications open around April–May through the Embassy/Consulates of Japan in India.
  • Intakes: April — main; September/October — many English programs.

Pros

  • Low national-university fees
  • MEXT is one of the most generous scholarships
  • Talent shortage in IT and engineering

Cons

  • Japanese language essential for most jobs
  • Work culture adjustments

FAQs: studying in Japan

How much does it cost to study in Japan for Indian students?

A master’s in Japan costs about ₹9.5 lakh – ₹19.3 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹19.0 lakh–₹38.6 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹9.5 lakh – ₹19.3 lakh a year. National universities charge a standard ¥535,800/year (+ ¥282,000 admission fee). Private universities ¥800,000–1,500,000+.

How much money do I need to show for a Japan student visa?

Show funds for tuition + living (often about ¥2–2.5 million for year one) to get a Certificate of Eligibility.

Can Indian students work part-time in Japan?

Up to 28 hrs/week with a “permission to engage in activity”; 40 hrs/week in long vacations.

How long can I stay in Japan after graduation?

Change to “Designated Activities” for up to 1 year of job hunting.

Can I get PR in Japan after studying?

Possible outlook. Highly Skilled Professional points can lead to PR in 1–3 years.

Do I need IELTS to study in Japan?

English-taught programs (G30 and others) ask IELTS 6.0–6.5; Japanese programs need JLPT N2/N1.

What are the intakes in Japan?

April — main. September/October — many English programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Japan?

MEXT (Japanese Government) Scholarship (UG/PG/PhD): Full tuition + monthly stipend + flights — apply via the Embassy of Japan in India. JASSO Honors Scholarship (UG/PG): Monthly stipend.
