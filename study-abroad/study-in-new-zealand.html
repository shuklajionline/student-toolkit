Study in New Zealand for Indian Students 2027

Safe, smaller country with up to 3 years post-study work.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in New Zealand costs an Indian student about ₹24.4 lakh – ₹40.5 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹22.9 lakh – ₹38.0 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 25 hours a week while studying and stay 3 years after graduation to work.

New Zealand student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Fee Paying Student Visa. Recent guides report fees from NZ$375 to NZ$850 — confirm on Immigration New Zealand.

Several weeks; apply online.

Money you must show

NZ$20,000 per year of study (or NZ$1,667 per month for shorter courses), plus tuition.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 25 hrs/week in term for eligible students (from 3 Nov 2025); full-time in scheduled breaks.

Post-study work

Post-study work visa up to 3 years for eligible bachelor’s and master’s graduates; PhD students’ partners can get open work visas.

Permanent residence

Moderate Skilled Migrant Category uses a points system linked to qualifications, salary and NZ work experience.

Language for jobs

English.

FAQs: studying in New Zealand

How much does it cost to study in New Zealand for Indian students?

A master’s in New Zealand costs about ₹24.4 lakh – ₹40.5 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1.5 years total: ₹36.6 lakh–₹60.8 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹22.9 lakh – ₹38.0 lakh a year. All 8 universities are ranked internationally; PhD students pay domestic fees (about NZ$7k–9k).

How much money do I need to show for a New Zealand student visa?

NZ$20,000 per year of study (or NZ$1,667 per month for shorter courses), plus tuition.

Can Indian students work part-time in New Zealand?

Up to 25 hrs/week in term for eligible students (from 3 Nov 2025); full-time in scheduled breaks.

How long can I stay in New Zealand after graduation?

Post-study work visa up to 3 years for eligible bachelor’s and master’s graduates; PhD students’ partners can get open work visas.

Can I get PR in New Zealand after studying?

Moderate outlook. Skilled Migrant Category uses a points system linked to qualifications, salary and NZ work experience.

Do I need IELTS to study in New Zealand?

IELTS 6.0–6.5 typical.

What are the intakes in New Zealand?

February — main intake. July — second intake.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in New Zealand?

University international excellence awards (UG/PG): NZ$5,000–10,000 off first-year tuition. Doctoral scholarships (PhD): Stipend + fees.
