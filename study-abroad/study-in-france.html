Study in France for Indian Students 2027

Low public fees, top business schools, special post-study terms for Indians.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in France costs an Indian student about ₹13.8 lakh – ₹57.9 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹12.7 lakh – ₹32.4 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 2 years after graduation to work.

France student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

VLS-TS long-stay student visa (via Campus France India). Visa fee about €50–99 + Campus France processing fee (paid in INR).

Campus France interview + VFS; allow 4–8 weeks.

Money you must show

Commonly cited as €615 per month (€7,380 per year). Some 2026 guides report a higher figure for applications from August 2026 — confirm with Campus France India before applying.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university through Campus France India.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 964 hours per year (about 60% of full time).

Post-study work

Temporary residence permit (APS) to look for work. Under the India–France migration agreement, Indian master’s graduates can get a longer stay (up to 24 months) and a 5-year short-stay Schengen visa — check Campus France India.

Permanent residence

Moderate Talent Passport and employee permits; 10-year residence card after 5 years of legal stay.

Language for jobs

French for most bachelor’s and daily life; many master’s/MBA programs in English.

Things Indian students must know

  • Indian students apply through the Campus France India “Études en France” process before the visa.
  • French Embassy runs the Charpak program only for Indian nationals.
  • Intakes: September — main intake; January — some business school programs.

Pros

  • Low fees at public universities
  • World-class business schools
  • Generous post-study terms for Indian master’s graduates
  • Free student health coverage

Cons

  • French language needed for most jobs
  • Paris housing is expensive and scarce
  • Bureaucracy (OFII validation, CAF)

FAQs: studying in France

How much does it cost to study in France for Indian students?

A master’s in France costs about ₹13.8 lakh – ₹57.9 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹27.6 lakh–₹1.16 crore). A bachelor’s costs about ₹12.7 lakh – ₹32.4 lakh a year. Public universities: €2,895/year (licence) and €3,941/year (master) for non-EU students, and many universities waive these down to the EU rate (€178/€254). Grandes écoles and business schools charge €10k–40k+.

How much money do I need to show for a France student visa?

Commonly cited as €615 per month (€7,380 per year). Some 2026 guides report a higher figure for applications from August 2026 — confirm with Campus France India before applying.

Can Indian students work part-time in France?

Up to 964 hours per year (about 60% of full time).

How long can I stay in France after graduation?

Temporary residence permit (APS) to look for work. Under the India–France migration agreement, Indian master’s graduates can get a longer stay (up to 24 months) and a 5-year short-stay Schengen visa — check Campus France India.

Can I get PR in France after studying?

Moderate outlook. Talent Passport and employee permits; 10-year residence card after 5 years of legal stay.

Do I need IELTS to study in France?

English-taught programs ask IELTS 6.0–6.5; French programs need DELF/DALF (B2+).

What are the intakes in France?

September — main intake. January — some business school programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in France?

Charpak Scholarship (India-specific) (UG/PG): Tuition waiver/stipend for Indian students via the French Embassy. Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (PG/PhD): Monthly allowance + travel, nominated by institution. Business-school merit awards (PG): 10–50% fee reductions.
