Study in Italy for Indian Students 2027

Income-based public fees and regional scholarships that can cover living costs.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Italy costs an Indian student about ₹9.2 lakh – ₹18.9 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹9.2 lakh – ₹18.9 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Italy student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Type D study visa (pre-enrolment on Universitaly). About €50 visa fee + permesso di soggiorno costs after arrival.

Universitaly pre-enrolment, then VFS; allow 4–8 weeks.

Money you must show

About €6,950 per year (set yearly by the Ministry), plus proof of accommodation.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university and complete Universitaly pre-enrolment.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 20 hrs/week (1,040 hours/year).

Post-study work

Permit to stay up to 12 months after graduation to look for work.

Permanent residence

Possible EU long-term residence after 5 years; Italian helps a lot with jobs.

Language for jobs

Italian for most jobs; many English-taught master’s.

Things Indian students must know

  • DSU regional scholarships are income-based — collect certified income and property documents from India early.
  • Intakes: September/October — main intake; pre-enrolment usually by spring.

Pros

  • Very low public fees
  • Regional scholarships can cover living costs
  • Strong design, architecture and engineering schools
  • Rich culture

Cons

  • Italian needed for most jobs
  • Slow bureaucracy (permesso)
  • Lower salaries than northern Europe

FAQs: studying in Italy

How much does it cost to study in Italy for Indian students?

A master’s in Italy costs about ₹9.2 lakh – ₹18.9 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹18.5 lakh–₹37.8 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹9.2 lakh – ₹18.9 lakh a year. Public universities charge fees based on family income (ISEE parificato for non-EU students). Private universities like Bocconi charge €14k–20k+.

How much money do I need to show for a Italy student visa?

About €6,950 per year (set yearly by the Ministry), plus proof of accommodation.

Can Indian students work part-time in Italy?

Up to 20 hrs/week (1,040 hours/year).

How long can I stay in Italy after graduation?

Permit to stay up to 12 months after graduation to look for work.

Can I get PR in Italy after studying?

Possible outlook. EU long-term residence after 5 years; Italian helps a lot with jobs.

Do I need IELTS to study in Italy?

IELTS 6.0 typical for English-taught programs; MOI accepted by many.

What are the intakes in Italy?

September/October — main intake; pre-enrolment usually by spring.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Italy?

Regional DSU scholarships (DiSCo Lazio, ER.GO, EDISU Piemonte, etc.) (UG/PG): Up to about €5,000–7,000/year + fee waiver + meals, income-based. Invest Your Talent in Italy (PG): Scholarship + internship for selected countries. University merit scholarships (UG/PG): Fee waivers.
