Study in Germany for Indian Students 2027

Little or no tuition at public universities, 18-month job-seeker permit.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Germany costs an Indian student about ₹12.7 lakh – ₹23.7 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹12.7 lakh – ₹20.7 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 18 months after graduation to work.

Germany student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

National (D) visa for study. €75 visa fee + blocked-account provider fees.

Appointment waits in India can be long — book early through the German missions/VFS.

Money you must show

€11,904 per year (€992/month) in a blocked account for 2026, unless you have a scholarship or formal sponsor. The amount is reviewed yearly — check the Federal Foreign Office.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university and your APS certificate.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange the blocked account and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

140 full days or 280 half days per year; student-assistant (HiWi) jobs at the university don’t count toward the limit.

Post-study work

18-month residence permit to look for a job after graduation; you can work any job during that time.

Permanent residence

Strong Settlement permit possible after about 21–27 months on an EU Blue Card (faster with German B1), or after 2 years of skilled work for German graduates.

Language for jobs

German for most bachelor’s and many jobs; many master’s are English-taught.

Things Indian students must know

  • Indian applicants need an APS certificate (Academic Evaluation Centre, New Delhi) before applying for a student visa — allow 4–8 weeks.
  • Many universities use uni-assist for applications.
  • Indian bachelor’s graduates usually need a 3- or 4-year degree; 3-year degrees may need an extra year for some master’s.
  • Intakes: Winter semester (Oct) — apply roughly Dec–Jul 15; Summer semester (Apr) — apply roughly Jun–Jan 15.

Pros

  • Near-zero tuition at most public universities
  • 18-month job-seeker permit
  • Strong demand for engineers and IT skills
  • Clear path to permanent residence

Cons

  • German language needed for most jobs and daily life
  • APS + blocked account + visa appointment take time
  • Housing hard to find in big cities
  • Bureaucracy is slow

FAQs: studying in Germany

How much does it cost to study in Germany for Indian students?

A master’s in Germany costs about ₹12.7 lakh – ₹23.7 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹25.5 lakh–₹47.5 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹12.7 lakh – ₹20.7 lakh a year. Most public universities charge only a semester contribution (€150–400 per semester). Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students €1,500 per semester; TU Munich charges non-EU students tuition (about €2,000–6,000 per semester). Private universities €10k–20k+ per year.

How much money do I need to show for a Germany student visa?

€11,904 per year (€992/month) in a blocked account for 2026, unless you have a scholarship or formal sponsor. The amount is reviewed yearly — check the Federal Foreign Office.

Can Indian students work part-time in Germany?

140 full days or 280 half days per year; student-assistant (HiWi) jobs at the university don’t count toward the limit.

How long can I stay in Germany after graduation?

18-month residence permit to look for a job after graduation; you can work any job during that time.

Can I get PR in Germany after studying?

Strong outlook. Settlement permit possible after about 21–27 months on an EU Blue Card (faster with German B1), or after 2 years of skilled work for German graduates.

Do I need IELTS to study in Germany?

English-taught master’s ask IELTS 6.0–6.5; some accept MOI letters. Most bachelor’s programs are in German (C1/TestDaF/DSH needed).

What are the intakes in Germany?

Winter semester (Oct) — apply roughly Dec–Jul 15. Summer semester (Apr) — apply roughly Jun–Jan 15.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Germany?

DAAD scholarships (PG/PhD): Monthly stipend + insurance + travel allowance. Deutschlandstipendium (UG/PG): €300/month, merit-based. Heinrich Böll / Konrad Adenauer foundations (PG/PhD): Stipends for strong academic + social profiles.
