Study in South Korea for Indian Students 2027

Affordable tuition, fully funded GKS scholarships, strong tech universities.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in South Korea costs an Indian student about ₹12.2 lakh – ₹24.3 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹12.2 lakh – ₹24.3 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 25 hours a week while studying and stay 2 years after graduation to work.

South Korea student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

D-2 student visa. Check the Korean Embassy in India.

About 2–4 weeks.

Money you must show

Show about US$16,000–20,000 equivalent (varies by region and university).

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Part-time work with permission; hours depend on level and Korean ability (TOPIK).

Post-study work

D-10 job-seeker visa (6 months, extendable up to 2 years).

Permanent residence

Possible E-7 skilled work visa, then points-based F-2 residence.

Language for jobs

Korean important for jobs.

Cons

FAQs: studying in South Korea

How much does it cost to study in South Korea for Indian students?

A master’s in South Korea costs about ₹12.2 lakh – ₹24.3 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹24.4 lakh–₹48.6 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹12.2 lakh – ₹24.3 lakh a year. Public universities are cheaper; private ones (Yonsei, Korea University) cost more. Many universities give 30–100% scholarships to international students.

How much money do I need to show for a South Korea student visa?

Show about US$16,000–20,000 equivalent (varies by region and university).

Can Indian students work part-time in South Korea?

Part-time work with permission; hours depend on level and Korean ability (TOPIK).

How long can I stay in South Korea after graduation?

D-10 job-seeker visa (6 months, extendable up to 2 years).

Can I get PR in South Korea after studying?

Possible outlook. E-7 skilled work visa, then points-based F-2 residence.

Do I need IELTS to study in South Korea?

English programs ask IELTS 5.5–6.5; Korean programs need TOPIK 3–4+.

What are the intakes in South Korea?

March — main. September — second.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in South Korea?

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) (UG/PG): Full tuition + stipend + flights + Korean language year. KAIST/University scholarships (UG/PG): Full or partial tuition waivers.
