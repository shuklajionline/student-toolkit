Study in Sweden for Indian Students 2027

Innovation-focused universities and a 12-month job-seeker permit.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Sweden costs an Indian student about ₹18.4 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹17.5 lakh – ₹28.0 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Sweden student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Residence permit for studies. About SEK 1,500 application fee.

Apply early — processing can take 1–3 months.

Money you must show

Migrationsverket sets a monthly maintenance amount (around SEK 10,000+/month) — check the current figure.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

No fixed hour limit, but studies must come first.

Post-study work

Residence permit up to 12 months to look for work after graduation.

Permanent residence

Moderate Permanent residence after 4 years of work permit, with stricter rules introduced recently.

English programs common; Swedish helps for jobs.

Things Indian students must know

  • Applications go through one central portal (universityadmissions.se).

Pros

  • No fixed weekly work limit

Cons

  • High tuition for non-EU
  • Swedish needed for many jobs

FAQs: studying in Sweden

How much does it cost to study in Sweden for Indian students?

A master’s in Sweden costs about ₹18.4 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹36.8 lakh–₹83.9 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹17.5 lakh – ₹28.0 lakh a year. Tuition for non-EU students; varies by subject (architecture/medicine at the top).

How much money do I need to show for a Sweden student visa?

Migrationsverket sets a monthly maintenance amount (around SEK 10,000+/month) — check the current figure.

Can Indian students work part-time in Sweden?

No fixed hour limit, but studies must come first.

How long can I stay in Sweden after graduation?

Residence permit up to 12 months to look for work after graduation.

Can I get PR in Sweden after studying?

Moderate outlook. Permanent residence after 4 years of work permit, with stricter rules introduced recently.

Do I need IELTS to study in Sweden?

“English 6” level, usually IELTS 6.5 with no band below 5.5.

What are the intakes in Sweden?

August — main (central application via University Admissions, deadline mid-January). January — few programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Sweden?

Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SISGP) (PG): Tuition + living for leaders with work experience — India eligible. University tuition waivers (PG): 25–100% of tuition.
