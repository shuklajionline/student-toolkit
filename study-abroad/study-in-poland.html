Study in Poland for Indian Students 2027

One of the cheapest EU destinations for both tuition and living.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Poland costs an Indian student about ₹8.9 lakh – ₹17.8 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹8.4 lakh – ₹16.8 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work without a weekly cap as a full-time student while studying and stay 9 months after graduation to work.

Poland student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

National (D) visa for studies. About €80.

Consular appointments can be slow — apply early. Checks on admissions have tightened.

Money you must show

Show funds for living and return travel as required by the Polish consulate (modest compared with western Europe).

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Things Indian students must know

  • Students can work without a work permit while studying full-time.
  • Intakes: October — main; February — some programs.

Pros

  • Very low tuition and living costs
  • No work-hour limit for full-time students
  • Growing tech hubs (Kraków, Wrocław, Warsaw)

Cons

  • Polish needed for many jobs
  • Lower salaries
  • Visa appointment delays

FAQs: studying in Poland

How much does it cost to study in Poland for Indian students?

A master’s in Poland costs about ₹8.9 lakh – ₹17.8 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹17.9 lakh–₹35.6 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹8.4 lakh – ₹16.8 lakh a year. English-taught programs at public universities; medicine (English) €10k–16k/year.

How much money do I need to show for a Poland student visa?

Show funds for living and return travel as required by the Polish consulate (modest compared with western Europe).

Can Indian students work part-time in Poland?

Full-time students with a residence permit can work without a separate work permit.

How long can I stay in Poland after graduation?

Temporary residence permit up to 9 months to look for work after graduation.

Can I get PR in Poland after studying?

Possible outlook. EU long-term residence after 5 years.

Do I need IELTS to study in Poland?

IELTS 5.5–6.0 typical; MOI often accepted.

What are the intakes in Poland?

October — main. February — some programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Poland?

NAWA programmes (UG/PG/PhD): Check eligibility for Indian citizens each year. University scholarships (UG/PG): Rector’s scholarships for top students.
