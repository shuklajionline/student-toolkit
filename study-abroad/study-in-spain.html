Study in Spain for Indian Students 2027

Affordable Europe with work rights up to 30 hrs/week.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Spain costs an Indian student about ₹12.3 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹11.3 lakh – ₹26.6 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 30 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Spain student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student visa / stay permit. About €80 + TIE card after arrival.

About 1–2 months.

Money you must show

About 100% of IPREM per month (around €600/month) — check with the Spanish consulate.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

FAQs: studying in Spain

How much does it cost to study in Spain for Indian students?

A master’s in Spain costs about ₹12.3 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹12.3 lakh–₹41.9 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹11.3 lakh – ₹26.6 lakh a year. Public universities are inexpensive (higher fees for non-EU in some regions). Business schools (IE, ESADE, IESE) are expensive.

How much money do I need to show for a Spain student visa?

About 100% of IPREM per month (around €600/month) — check with the Spanish consulate.

Can Indian students work part-time in Spain?

Up to 30 hrs/week, compatible with studies (rules updated in 2025).

How long can I stay in Spain after graduation?

Up to 12 months to look for work or start a business after graduation.

Can I get PR in Spain after studying?

Possible outlook. Long-term residence after 5 years; Spanish is important for most jobs.

Do I need IELTS to study in Spain?

English-taught programs ask IELTS 6.0–6.5; Spanish programs need DELE B2.

What are the intakes in Spain?

September/October — main. January/February — some private programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Spain?

Business school merit scholarships (PG): 10–50% off tuition. University scholarships (UG/PG): Varies.
