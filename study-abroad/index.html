Study Abroad Country Guides

Honest, sourced guides for Indian students — what it really costs in rupees, visa and money rules, work rights and scholarships.

Not sure where to go? Use the Study Abroad Finder →

North America

Study in USA

₹29.9 lakh – ₹81.0 lakh / year

Top-ranked universities, STEM OPT up to 3 years, highest costs

Study in Canada

₹24.5 lakh – ₹51.0 lakh / year

PGWP up to 3 years and established PR routes — now with study-permit caps

Europe

Study in UK

₹35.1 lakh – ₹71.6 lakh / year

1-year master’s, Graduate Route work visa, strong global brand

Study in Germany

₹12.7 lakh – ₹23.7 lakh / year

Little or no tuition at public universities, 18-month job-seeker permit

Study in Ireland

₹25.2 lakh – ₹51.9 lakh / year

English-speaking EU country with big tech and pharma employers

Study in France

₹13.8 lakh – ₹57.9 lakh / year

Low public fees, top business schools, special post-study terms for Indians

Study in Netherlands

₹25.1 lakh – ₹44.5 lakh / year

Hundreds of English-taught degrees and a 1-year orientation visa

Study in Italy

₹9.2 lakh – ₹18.9 lakh / year

Income-based public fees and regional scholarships that can cover living costs

Study in Spain

₹12.3 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh / year

Affordable Europe with work rights up to 30 hrs/week

Study in Sweden

₹18.4 lakh – ₹41.9 lakh / year

Innovation-focused universities and a 12-month job-seeker permit

Study in Finland

₹18.4 lakh – ₹35.5 lakh / year

High-quality education, 2-year post-study permit, scholarships common

Study in Denmark

₹20.6 lakh – ₹38.6 lakh / year

Practical, project-based learning and a 3-year job-search card

Study in Norway

₹25.6 lakh – ₹51.5 lakh / year

No longer tuition-free for non-EU students; very high living costs

Study in Poland

₹8.9 lakh – ₹17.8 lakh / year

One of the cheapest EU destinations for both tuition and living

Study in Austria

₹13.9 lakh – ₹18.3 lakh / year

Low public tuition (about €1,450/year) and high quality of life

Study in Switzerland

₹26.7 lakh – ₹44.4 lakh / year

World-class ETH/EPFL at modest fees — but the highest living costs

Oceania

Study in Australia

₹31.5 lakh – ₹51.8 lakh / year

Generous work hours, 2–3 year graduate visa, high visa fees

Study in New Zealand

₹24.4 lakh – ₹40.5 lakh / year

Safe, smaller country with up to 3 years post-study work

Asia & Middle East

Study in Singapore

₹31.2 lakh – ₹65.8 lakh / year

Asia’s top universities 4 hours from India — no post-study work visa

Study in Japan

₹9.5 lakh – ₹19.3 lakh / year

Low national-university fees, MEXT scholarships and a tech job market short of talent

Study in South Korea

₹12.2 lakh – ₹24.3 lakh / year

Affordable tuition, fully funded GKS scholarships, strong tech universities

Study in UAE (Dubai)

₹21.0 lakh – ₹45.0 lakh / year

Branch campuses of UK, Australian and Indian universities, 3 hours from home

Study in Malaysia

₹9.7 lakh – ₹19.8 lakh / year

Low-cost UK/Australian branch-campus degrees in an English-friendly country

Study in Hong Kong

₹28.0 lakh – ₹64.4 lakh / year

Top-ranked universities and a 2-year IANG post-study visa

MBBS abroad

MBBS in Russia

₹5.3 lakh – ₹10.4 lakh / year

Low-cost 6-year MBBS with long history of Indian students — check NMC compliance

MBBS in Georgia

₹7.3 lakh – ₹12.0 lakh / year

English-medium MD programs in a safe, Europe-facing country

MBBS in Kazakhstan

₹5.3 lakh – ₹9.1 lakh / year

Affordable MBBS — but verify program length and internship meet NMC rules

MBBS in Uzbekistan

₹4.4 lakh – ₹8.1 lakh / year

Fast-growing low-cost MBBS destination — check NMC rules and clinical exposure
