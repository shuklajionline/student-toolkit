Study in UAE (Dubai) for Indian Students 2027

Branch campuses of UK, Australian and Indian universities, 3 hours from home.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in UAE (Dubai) costs an Indian student about ₹21.0 lakh – ₹45.0 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹18.6 lakh – ₹40.2 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 15 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

UAE (Dubai) student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Student residence visa (university-sponsored). Usually charged by the university (AED 3,000–6,000 including Emirates ID and medical).

About 2–4 weeks.

Money you must show

Student residence visa is sponsored by the university; bank statements may be requested.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Part-time work and internships are allowed with a permit, subject to university approval.

Post-study work

Graduates can switch to an employment visa; outstanding graduates may qualify for a Golden Visa.

Permanent residence

Limited No standard PR route; long-term Golden Visa for qualifying graduates and professionals.

Language for jobs

English widely used in business.

FAQs: studying in UAE (Dubai)

How much does it cost to study in UAE (Dubai) for Indian students?

A master’s in UAE (Dubai) costs about ₹21.0 lakh – ₹45.0 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1.5 years total: ₹31.5 lakh–₹67.5 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹18.6 lakh – ₹40.2 lakh a year. Dubai and Abu Dhabi host branch campuses (Heriot-Watt, Middlesex, Wollongong, BITS Pilani, Manipal, Amity, etc.) that award home-country degrees.

How much money do I need to show for a UAE (Dubai) student visa?

Student residence visa is sponsored by the university; bank statements may be requested.

Can Indian students work part-time in UAE (Dubai)?

Part-time work and internships are allowed with a permit, subject to university approval.

How long can I stay in UAE (Dubai) after graduation?

Graduates can switch to an employment visa; outstanding graduates may qualify for a Golden Visa.

Can I get PR in UAE (Dubai) after studying?

Limited outlook. No standard PR route; long-term Golden Visa for qualifying graduates and professionals.

Do I need IELTS to study in UAE (Dubai)?

IELTS 5.5–6.5; Indian board English often accepted.

What are the intakes in UAE (Dubai)?

September — main. January/February — many programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in UAE (Dubai)?

University merit scholarships (UG/PG): 10–50% off tuition common. NYU Abu Dhabi / Khalifa full scholarships (UG): Highly competitive, full funding.
