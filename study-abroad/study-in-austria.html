Study in Austria for Indian Students 2027

Low public tuition (about €1,450/year) and high quality of life.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Austria costs an Indian student about ₹13.9 lakh – ₹18.3 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹13.9 lakh – ₹18.3 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 20 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Public universities charge non-EU students about €726.72 per semester + a small student union fee. Private universities and MBA programs cost more. Vienna is the most expensive, but cheaper than Munich or Zurich. Rupee figures use 1 EUR ≈ ₹102; change the rate in the cost calculator.

Austria student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Residence permit “Student”. About €160 + card fee; check migration.gv.at.

Can take up to 3 months — apply early.

Money you must show

Monthly amount set by law (higher if you are over 24) — check the OeAD / migration.gv.at figure.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

FAQs: studying in Austria

How much does it cost to study in Austria for Indian students?

A master’s in Austria costs about ₹13.9 lakh – ₹18.3 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹27.8 lakh–₹36.5 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹13.9 lakh – ₹18.3 lakh a year. Public universities charge non-EU students about €726.72 per semester + a small student union fee. Private universities and MBA programs cost more.

How much money do I need to show for a Austria student visa?

Monthly amount set by law (higher if you are over 24) — check the OeAD / migration.gv.at figure.

Can Indian students work part-time in Austria?

Up to 20 hrs/week with a work permit arranged by the employer.

How long can I stay in Austria after graduation?

Up to 12 months to look for a job after graduation.

Can I get PR in Austria after studying?

Possible outlook. Red-White-Red Card for graduates; long-term residence after 5 years.

Do I need IELTS to study in Austria?

IELTS 6.0–6.5 for English master’s; German needed for most bachelor’s.

What are the intakes in Austria?

October (winter) — main. March (summer).

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Austria?

OeAD / Ernst Mach grants (PG/PhD): Monthly stipends for selected countries. University scholarships (PG): Limited.
