Study in Finland for Indian Students 2027

High-quality education, 2-year post-study permit, scholarships common.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Finland costs an Indian student about ₹18.4 lakh – ₹35.5 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹18.4 lakh – ₹33.5 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 30 hours a week while studying and stay 2 years after graduation to work.

Finland student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

Residence permit for studies. About €350 (online) — check Migri.

Usually 1–2 months.

Money you must show

About €800 per month (€9,600 per year), unless covered by a scholarship.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 30 hrs/week in term; full-time in holidays.

Post-study work

Residence permit up to 2 years to look for work or start a business.

Permanent residence

Moderate Permanent residence after 4 years of continuous residence (rules tightened recently).

Language for jobs

English programs; Finnish/Swedish useful for jobs.

FAQs: studying in Finland

How much does it cost to study in Finland for Indian students?

A master’s in Finland costs about ₹18.4 lakh – ₹35.5 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (2 years total: ₹36.7 lakh–₹71.0 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹18.4 lakh – ₹33.5 lakh a year. Most universities offer tuition scholarships (25–100%) to strong non-EU applicants.

How much money do I need to show for a Finland student visa?

About €800 per month (€9,600 per year), unless covered by a scholarship.

Can Indian students work part-time in Finland?

Up to 30 hrs/week in term; full-time in holidays.

How long can I stay in Finland after graduation?

Residence permit up to 2 years to look for work or start a business.

Can I get PR in Finland after studying?

Moderate outlook. Permanent residence after 4 years of continuous residence (rules tightened recently).

Do I need IELTS to study in Finland?

IELTS 6.5 typical for master’s; 6.0 at universities of applied sciences.

What are the intakes in Finland?

August/September — main (joint application in January). January — some programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Finland?

University tuition scholarships (UG/PG): 50–100% fee waivers, renewable on progress. Finland Scholarship (UG/PG): Tuition + €5,000 relocation (selected universities).
