Study in Netherlands for Indian Students 2027

Hundreds of English-taught degrees and a 1-year orientation visa.

By the Student Toolkit editorial team · Sources linked below · Editorial policy

Quick answer: Studying in Netherlands costs an Indian student about ₹25.1 lakh – ₹44.5 lakh per year for a master’s and ₹22.0 lakh – ₹39.4 lakh per year for a bachelor’s, including living costs. You can work 16 hours a week while studying and stay 1 year after graduation to work.

Netherlands student visa for Indian students

Visa & fees

MVV + residence permit (applied by your university). Fee paid via the university (about €200+); check IND.

Usually 2–4 weeks once the university submits.

Money you must show

The IND sets a monthly “norm amount” for students (about €1,000+/month) that your university checks before applying for your permit.

Education loan sanction letters are usually accepted as proof of funds. Compare lenders in our education loan guide.

Steps

  1. Get an offer from a recognised university.
  2. Pay the deposit and receive your admission/invitation letter.
  3. Arrange proof of funds and health insurance.
  4. Apply for the visa online or at VFS/embassy and give biometrics.
  5. Book accommodation and flights; complete registration after arrival.

Work during and after studies

Part-time work while studying

Up to 16 hrs/week in term or full-time in June–August, with a work permit arranged by the employer.

Post-study work

Orientation Year (zoekjaar) — 12 months to find work, at a lower salary threshold for the highly skilled migrant permit.

Permanent residence

Moderate Permanent residence or naturalisation after 5 years; highly skilled migrant route is common.

Language for jobs

English widely spoken; Dutch helps for jobs.

Things Indian students must know

  • Non-EU application deadlines are often months earlier than EU deadlines.
  • Start your housing search the day you are admitted.
  • Intakes: September — main (non-EU deadlines often Jan–Apr); February — some programs.

Pros

Cons

FAQs: studying in Netherlands

How much does it cost to study in Netherlands for Indian students?

A master’s in Netherlands costs about ₹25.1 lakh – ₹44.5 lakh a year including tuition, living and insurance (1 year total: ₹25.1 lakh–₹44.5 lakh). A bachelor’s costs about ₹22.0 lakh – ₹39.4 lakh a year. Research universities and universities of applied sciences set their own non-EU fees. The government has been cutting back some English-taught bachelor’s programs.

How much money do I need to show for a Netherlands student visa?

The IND sets a monthly “norm amount” for students (about €1,000+/month) that your university checks before applying for your permit.

Can Indian students work part-time in Netherlands?

Up to 16 hrs/week in term or full-time in June–August, with a work permit arranged by the employer.

How long can I stay in Netherlands after graduation?

Orientation Year (zoekjaar) — 12 months to find work, at a lower salary threshold for the highly skilled migrant permit.

Can I get PR in Netherlands after studying?

Moderate outlook. Permanent residence or naturalisation after 5 years; highly skilled migrant route is common.

Do I need IELTS to study in Netherlands?

IELTS 6.0–6.5 (applied sciences) to 6.5–7.0 (research universities).

What are the intakes in Netherlands?

September — main (non-EU deadlines often Jan–Apr). February — some programs.

Which scholarships can Indian students get in Netherlands?

Orange Tulip Scholarship (India) (UG/PG): Partial to full tuition at participating institutions. NL Scholarship (formerly Holland Scholarship) (UG/PG): €5,000 in the first year. University excellence scholarships (PG): Partial to full fee waivers.
