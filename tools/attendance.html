FAQ

Everything about attendance

Common questions students ask about attendance calculations.

How is attendance percentage calculated?

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Effective Conducted) × 100. Effective conducted classes exclude neutral sessions where no attendance was marked. For example: 30 attended out of 35 effective = 85.7%.

How many classes can I skip and still stay at 75%?

Safe Bunks = floor((Attended − 0.75 × Effective) ÷ 0.75). If this is zero or negative, you cannot skip any more classes. Our calculator shows this instantly. The formula assumes you attend all remaining classes and only counts how many you can safely miss from here.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

If your attendance is below the required threshold, use: Classes Needed = ceil((T% × Effective − Attended) ÷ (1 − T%)), where T% is your minimum threshold (e.g. 0.75). This gives the minimum consecutive classes to attend without skipping any more.

What is the minimum attendance in most colleges?

Most colleges and universities require a minimum of 75% attendance to be eligible for final exams. Some require 80% or higher. Our calculator supports 70%, 75%, and 80% thresholds — always verify with your institution's official policy.

What are neutral classes?

Neutral classes are sessions that were held but where attendance was not formally recorded — such as cancelled classes still marked as conducted, or special exempt sessions. These are excluded from the effective count so your percentage is calculated accurately.
