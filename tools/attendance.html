The 75% Attendance Rule in Indian Colleges

Most Indian universities — including DU, Mumbai University, VTU, Anna University, JNTU, and colleges affiliated to UGC — mandate a minimum of 75% attendance for a student to be eligible to appear in semester exams. Some institutes like medical colleges, NITs, and IITs require 80% or even 85%.

Falling below the minimum can result in detention from exams, loss of internal assessment marks, cancellation of scholarships, or being marked short-attendance in your academic record. Our calculator supports all three common thresholds — 75%, 80%, and 85% — as well as a custom target for your specific institution.

Always verify the exact policy with your college's examination department, as individual departments may impose stricter requirements than the university default.

How Attendance Percentage is Calculated

The standard formula is straightforward: Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100. For example, if your college has held 120 classes so far and you attended 94, your attendance is (94 ÷ 120) × 100 = 78.3% — safely above the 75% rule.

To find how many more classes you can bunk while staying above 75%, the formula is: Safe Bunks = floor(Attended ÷ 0.75) − Total. Using the same example: floor(94 ÷ 0.75) − 120 = 125 − 120 = 5 bunks remaining.

If your attendance has already dropped below 75%, our Recovery Mode calculates exactly how many consecutive classes you need to attend without a single absence to get back above the threshold.

What Are Ghost Classes?

Ghost classes (also called cancelled classes or proxy classes) are sessions that were officially scheduled and counted in your college's total class tally — but never actually conducted. Common examples include classes where the professor was absent, last-minute cancellations, or sessions marked as held during college events. Since you couldn't attend something that didn't happen, our calculator lets you subtract these from your total to get your real, fair attendance percentage.
