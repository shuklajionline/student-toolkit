Everything You Need to Know About College Attendance in India

From UGC guidelines to university-specific rules — understand how attendance affects your exams, placements, and degree.

📋

UGC 75% Attendance Rule

The University Grants Commission mandates 75% minimum attendance for all undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Indian universities. Falling below can result in exam detention. Some universities offer 5-10% condonation with medical certificates.

🏥

Medical Condonation Policies

Most Indian colleges allow 5-15% attendance relaxation for genuine medical reasons. You'll need certificates from registered medical practitioners. Some universities like DU and Mumbai University have specific forms for this process.

🎓

Placement & Attendance Link

Many companies check attendance records during campus placements. Consistent attendance above 85% is viewed positively. Some colleges require minimum 75% attendance to be eligible for placement drives.

⚖️

State University Variations

Different states have different rules: Maharashtra (75%), Tamil Nadu (75% with 10% condonation), Karnataka (85% for professional courses), Delhi (66% minimum). Always check your specific university guidelines.

💡

Smart Attendance Management

Plan your absences strategically. Use our Bunk Meter to know exactly how many classes you can skip. Track multiple subjects separately. Set recovery goals when you fall behind. Small consistent attendance beats last-minute cramming.

📱

Digital Attendance Systems

Modern colleges use biometric, RFID, or app-based attendance. These systems update in real-time. Our calculator works with any system — just enter your numbers from the portal or app to track your standing.
