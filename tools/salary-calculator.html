📐 Formulas We Use

📊 Attendance %

% = (Attended ÷ Total) × 100

With ghost classes: % = Attended ÷ (Total − Ghost) × 100

🎯 Bunkable Classes

Bunks = (Attended ÷ Target%) × 100 − Total

How many future classes you can miss and still maintain your target attendance.

🚨 Recovery Needed

Attend = (Target% × Total − Attended) ÷ (1 − Target%)

How many consecutive classes you must attend to reach your target percentage.

Ghost classes are classes that never actually happened — teacher was absent, holiday declared late, class cancelled — but your college still counts them in "total classes held." Our ghost class feature subtracts these to give you your REAL attendance percentage.

Most Indian universities require 75% minimum attendance to sit for exams. Below that you face: detention (barred from exams), loss of internal marks, scholarship cancellation, or parent notification. Some colleges allow medical certificates. Always check your university's specific rules.
