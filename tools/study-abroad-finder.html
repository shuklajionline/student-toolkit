How to choose a country to study abroad

1. Start with total cost, not tuition

Tuition is only part of the bill. Living costs, health insurance, visa fees and flights often equal or exceed tuition — in Germany tuition can be near zero but you still need €11,904 a year for living. Compare the yearly totals in rupees above.

2. Check work rights after graduation

A post-study work visa is how most students repay loans and gain international experience. USA: 3 years; Canada: 3 years; New Zealand: 3 years; Denmark: 3 years; Australia: 2 years. Singapore and Malaysia have no dedicated post-study work visa.

3. Be honest about language

English-taught degrees are common across Europe and Asia, but part-time and graduate jobs in Germany, France, Italy, Japan or Korea usually need the local language. Start learning (A2–B1) before you fly if you want to work there.

4. Match the country to your course

Engineering and research → Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland. Business and finance → UK, USA, France, Singapore, Hong Kong. Health and nursing → Canada, Australia, UK, Ireland. Design → Italy, UK, Netherlands. Use the “Strong in” filter.

5. Plan money rules from India

Education loan interest is tax-deductible. Interest on an education loan can be deducted under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act for up to 8 years (no upper limit on the amount).

TCS on money sent abroad for education. Under LRS, remittances for education funded by a loan from a specified financial institution attract no TCS. Self-funded education remittances above ₹10 lakh a year attract TCS (5% on the amount above ₹10 lakh). TCS can be claimed back when you file your ITR.

Collateral-free loan limits. Public-sector banks typically lend up to about ₹7.5 lakh without collateral; NBFCs and some banks offer more (often ₹40–75 lakh+) for top universities, at higher interest.

Register on MADAD. The Government of India’s MADAD portal lets Indian students abroad register and request consular help.

Forex & cards. Use a forex card or student account for living costs; wire tuition through your bank (A2 form) for lower charges.

6. Avoid common traps

  • Never pay for “guaranteed” admission or visas.
  • Check the university is recognised (and NMC-compliant for MBBS).
  • Older blogs say Norway is free — it isn’t for non-EU students since 2023.
  • Rules change every year; confirm on the official links in each guide.

Study abroad without IELTS

Universities in these countries often accept a waiver (high Class 12 English or MOI) or an alternative test: UK, Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, UAE (Dubai), Malaysia.

Waivers are decided by each university and some visas still need a test score.

Study abroad FAQs for Indian students

Which is the cheapest country to study abroad for Indian students?

Counting tuition, living costs and insurance, the cheapest options for a master’s are Poland (from about ₹8.9 lakh a year), Italy (from about ₹9.2 lakh a year), Japan (from about ₹9.5 lakh a year), Malaysia (from about ₹9.7 lakh a year). Germany’s public universities charge little or no tuition, but you must show €11,904 in a blocked account for living costs.

How much does it cost to study abroad from India in total?

A 1–2 year master’s typically costs ₹8–25 lakh in low-tuition Europe (Germany, Italy, France, Poland, Austria), ₹25–50 lakh in the UK, Ireland, Canada or Australia, and ₹40–90 lakh+ in the USA, including living costs. Use the cost calculator above to get a figure for your exact course, city and scholarship.

Which countries give the longest post-study work visa?

United States (3 years), Canada (3 years), New Zealand (3 years), Denmark (3 years), Australia (2 years), Ireland (2 years). From 1 January 2027 the UK Graduate Route drops from 2 years to 18 months for new applications.

Can I study abroad without IELTS?

Yes, in many cases. Universities in UK, Germany, Ireland, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Singapore and others can waive IELTS if your Class 12 English marks are high or you studied in English (MOI letter). Others accept Duolingo, PTE or TOEFL instead. Visa rules can still require a test (Australia, for example), so check each university and visa page.

Which country is best for PR after studying?

Canada and Germany currently offer the clearest study-to-permanent-residence routes for Indian students, followed by Australia, New Zealand and Ireland. Rules tightened in 2024–2026 everywhere, so PR is never guaranteed.

Can I work part-time while studying abroad?

Most countries allow about 20 hours a week during term: UK 20, USA 20 (on-campus), Canada 24, Australia 48 per fortnight, New Zealand 25, Germany 140 full days a year, France 964 hours a year, Spain 30 hours a week. Part-time income helps with living costs but rarely covers tuition.

How much money do I need to show for a student visa?

Examples for 2027 intakes: Germany €11,904 per year in a blocked account; Canada C$23,448 plus tuition; Australia A$29,710 plus tuition and travel; UK £1,483/month in London or £1,136/month elsewhere (up to 9 months) plus fees; Ireland €10,000 plus fees. Education loan sanction letters are accepted as proof in most countries.

Is MBBS abroad valid in India?

Only if the course meets the National Medical Commission’s Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate rules: at least 54 months of study in English, a 12-month internship at the same institution, and eligibility for a medical licence in that country. You must have qualified NEET-UG, and you must pass the screening/licensing exam (FMGE/NExT) back in India.

Is this data accurate?

Do I need a consultant to study abroad?

No. Every step — university applications, scholarships, visa, blocked account, loans — can be done yourself using the official links on each country guide. If you do use an agent, check what commission they earn from universities and never pay for “guaranteed” admission or visas.
