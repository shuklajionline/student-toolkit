Everything about resume scoring

What is an ATS score and why does it matter?

Most large companies use ATS (Applicant Tracking System) software to filter resumes before any human sees them. The software scans for keywords, structure, and formatting. Resumes scoring below ~60% are often auto-rejected. Our checker simulates this scan and tells you exactly what to fix.

What score should I aim for before applying?

Aim for 80+ before submitting. 80–89 is competitive and will pass most ATS filters. 90+ is excellent — top 5% of applicants. Scores below 65 carry real rejection risk from automated screening. Fix the specific issues flagged and re-analyze until you hit 80+.

How do I improve my score quickly?

Three fastest wins: (1) Add numbers to every achievement — "increased sales" becomes "increased sales by 35% in Q3". (2) Start every bullet point with an action verb (Led, Built, Optimized, Delivered). (3) Add the top 5–8 missing keywords from the keyword analysis section into your skills or bullet points naturally.
