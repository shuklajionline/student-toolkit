Common Questions

What if I'm not good at studies? +
You don't need 90% marks to have a great career! Many fields like hospitality, design, sports, skilled trades (electrician, plumbing), retail management, and entrepreneurship value skills and personality over marks. Our tool shows options specifically for average and below-average scorers too.
I have no idea what I want to do. Will this help? +
Absolutely. Just be honest about what subjects you can tolerate and what kind of activities you enjoy. The tool will match you with careers you might never have heard of — many students discover options they didn't know existed.
Which career pays the most in India? +
Top-paying careers include Software Engineering (₹8-50 LPA), Medicine (₹8-30 LPA), Chartered Accountancy (₹8-40 LPA), MBA from top colleges (₹12-60 LPA), Investment Banking, and Data Science. But remember — high salary often means high stress and long hours. Balance matters too.
Is this really free? How does it work without AI? +
100% free, forever. We use a smart matching system based on real career data, salary surveys, and educational requirements in India — no AI needed. Your inputs are matched against our database of 50+ careers with real-world information.
What if I dropped out or have a gap year? +
That's completely fine. Choose the "Dropped out / Gap year" option. We'll show you careers that don't need traditional degrees — like diploma courses, skill-based jobs, entrepreneurship, government jobs (some don't need degrees), and vocational training paths.
