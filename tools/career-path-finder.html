Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best career after 12th PCM in India? +
After 12th PCM, top careers include Engineering (B.Tech/BE), Architecture, Data Science, Merchant Navy, and Defence. With JEE preparation, IIT/NIT admissions open doors to top tech companies with ₹10-50 LPA packages. Our AI tool analyses your specific interests within PCM to give you personalised recommendations.
What are the best careers after 12th Commerce without Maths? +
Commerce without Maths opens doors to CA (Chartered Accountancy), Company Secretary, BBA, Hotel Management, Fashion Design, Mass Communication, and Law (BA LLB). Many of these careers offer excellent salary growth and are in high demand across India.
Is career guidance from AI reliable? +
Our AI analyses your inputs against real Indian career data including salary ranges, job demand, and educational requirements. It's a starting point for exploration — not a final decision. We recommend using these results to research further and speak with professionals in fields that interest you.
Which careers have highest salary in India for freshers? +
In 2025-26, highest paying fresher careers in India include Software Engineering (₹4-20 LPA), Investment Banking (₹6-15 LPA), Data Science (₹5-12 LPA), IAS/IPS (₹7-10 LPA + perks), Medicine (after residency: ₹8-20 LPA), and CA (₹6-15 LPA).
What if I don't know what I want to do? +
That's completely normal — most students don't! Just fill in what you enjoy doing, not what you think you should do. Our AI is specifically designed to help undecided students discover careers they might never have considered. Try multiple combinations and see what resonates.
