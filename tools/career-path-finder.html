Frequently Asked Questions

Best careers after 12th PCM in India?+
After PCM, top careers include Software Engineering (JEE → IIT/NIT, ₹8-50 LPA), Data Science, Architecture, Merchant Navy, and Defence (NDA). IIT placements average ₹20+ LPA at top companies.
Best careers after 12th PCB?+
PCB students can pursue MBBS (NEET → AIIMS/JIPMER), BDS, BAMS, Pharmacy, Nursing, Physiotherapy, or Biotechnology. MBBS post-residency earns ₹8-25 LPA.
Best commerce careers in India?+
CA (Chartered Accountancy) offers ₹8-40 LPA. Other top paths: CS, CMA, BBA→MBA (IIM via CAT), Banking, Law (BA LLB), and Mass Communication.
Is UPSC worth it for Arts students?+
IAS/IPS earn ₹7-10 LPA plus perks, housing and immense social status. Arts students often excel in UPSC. Other strong paths: Law, Journalism, Psychology, Teaching (NET/SET).
