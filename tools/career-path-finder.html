Frequently Asked Questions

Best careers after 12th PCM in India?+
After PCM, top careers include Software Engineering (JEE → IIT/NIT, ₹8-50 LPA), Data Science, Architecture (JEE Paper 2), Merchant Navy, and Defence (NDA). Our AI recommends the best match based on your specific interests within PCM.
What are the best careers after 12th PCB?+
PCB students can pursue MBBS (NEET, top colleges: AIIMS, JIPMER), BDS, BAMS, Pharmacy (B.Pharm), Nursing, Physiotherapy, or Biotechnology. MBBS remains the most sought-after with ₹8-20 LPA post-residency.
Best commerce careers in India?+
CA (Chartered Accountancy) offers the highest earning potential at ₹8-40 LPA. Other top paths include CS (Company Secretary), CMA, BBA→MBA (CAT for IIMs), Banking/Finance, Law (BA LLB), and Mass Communication.
Is UPSC worth it for Arts students?+
UPSC is highly competitive but extremely rewarding — IAS/IPS earn ₹7-10 LPA plus perks, housing, and social status. Arts students often excel due to strong GS exposure. Other strong options: Law (LLB/BA LLB), Journalism, Psychology, Teaching (NET/SET), and Social Work.
How accurate is AI career guidance?+
Our AI analyses your profile against real Indian career data including salary ranges, job demand, and entrance requirements. It's an excellent starting point — use results to research further, talk to professionals in your chosen field, and consult a career counsellor if needed.
