What Makes a Resume ATS-Friendly?

Most Indian companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen resumes before a recruiter reads them. ATS systems struggle with multi-column layouts, tables, graphics, and unusual fonts. All three templates in this generator use clean, parseable formatting with standard section headings that ATS scanners recognise — so your resume always gets through to a human.

Resume Tips for Indian Students & Freshers

Keep your resume to one page if you have under 2 years of experience. Lead with education if you're from a reputed institute. Include your CGPA if it's above 7.0. Highlight projects and internships prominently — these matter as much as jobs for freshers. Use action verbs like "Developed", "Built", "Achieved" and quantify your impact wherever possible. Avoid including a photo, date of birth, or father's name.
