About This College Predictor

This tool uses actual cutoff data from the 2025–26 admissions cycle to estimate your college chances. Cutoffs shown are for the General category — OBC, SC, ST and EWS cutoffs are typically 5–15 percentile points lower depending on the institution.

How CUET College Predictor Works

CUET (Common University Entrance Test) is conducted by NTA for admissions to central universities and several private universities. For BBA admissions at Delhi University colleges like SSCBS and DDU, CUET percentile is the primary criteria. Enter your expected CUET percentile and select General or OBC/SC/ST category to see which DU, JNVU, BHU, and other central university BBA and B.Com programmes are within reach.

How CAT College Predictor Works

CAT (Common Admission Test) is used for MBA/PGDM admissions at IIMs and hundreds of other management institutes. IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Bangalore require 99+ percentile for General category. FMS Delhi, India's best ROI MBA, requires 98 percentile. IIM Indore and MDI Gurgaon are accessible at 95–96 percentile. The predictor shows realistic chances based on IIM cutoffs published after each CAT cycle.

CUET BBA Cutoffs 2025–26

SSCBS Delhi (India's best BBA ROI): 95+ percentile General, 88+ OBC. DDU Delhi: 88–92 percentile General. For most other central university BBA programmes, 75–85 percentile is competitive. Private colleges using CUET scores: Christ University, Symbiosis (uses SET separately), Amity, and others.

CAT MBA Cutoffs 2025–26

IIM Ahmedabad: 99+ General. IIM Bangalore: 99+. IIM Calcutta: 99+. FMS Delhi: 98+. IIM Lucknow: 97+. IIM Kozhikode: 96+. MDI Gurgaon: 95+. IIM Indore: 95+. SPJIMR Mumbai: 95+. JBIMS Mumbai (MH-CET, not CAT): Maharashtra domicile with high MH-CET score. NMIMS Mumbai (NMAT, not CAT): 210+ NMAT score.

Frequently Asked Questions

What CUET percentile do I need for SSCBS Delhi BBA?
SSCBS Delhi requires 95+ percentile for General category and approximately 88+ for OBC-NCL. The cutoff varies slightly each year based on the number of applicants and seat availability. SSCBS also requires Maths as a subject in Class 12 — students without Maths are ineligible for BBA (FIA).
Which MBA colleges accept below 90 percentile CAT?
Several good MBA programmes accept below 90 percentile: NMIMS Mumbai (uses NMAT, not CAT), JBIMS Mumbai (uses MH-CET for Maharashtra students), Great Lakes Chennai, IMT Ghaziabad, FORE School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute, and many AICTE-approved private colleges. For IIMs, even the newer IIMs require 90+ percentile.
Is the college predictor accurate?
The predictor uses actual published cutoffs from 2025–26 admissions. However, cutoffs change slightly every year based on the number of applicants, paper difficulty, and seat availability. Use the results as a realistic guide, not a guarantee. Always check official college websites for the most current cutoff data before applying.
Does category affect college eligibility significantly?
Yes, significantly. For IIMs, OBC-NCL cutoffs are typically 5–7 percentile points lower than General. SC/ST cutoffs are 15–25 percentile points lower. For CUET-based admissions, similar relaxations apply at all central universities. EWS cutoffs are typically same as OBC-NCL. Always enter your correct category for accurate predictions.
