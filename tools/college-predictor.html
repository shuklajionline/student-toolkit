How the College Predictor Works

Our predictor uses a three-tier system to show your admission chances:

  • ✅ Safe (Green): Your percentile is 3+ points above the cutoff. High probability of getting a call/shortlist. Apply with confidence.
  • ⚡ Moderate (Amber): Your percentile is within 0–3 points of the cutoff. Competitive — apply strategically. Your academic profile, work experience, and diversity factors will matter.
  • 🎯 Reach (Red): Your percentile is up to 3 points below the cutoff. Low probability but worth applying to — cutoffs fluctuate 1–3 points each year based on application volume.

Cutoffs shown are the minimum percentile for shortlisting — not guaranteed admission. IIMs, top NLUs, and top NITs have multi-stage selection processes after the initial percentile cutoff.

Category-Wise Cutoff Relaxation

All cutoffs in the predictor adjust based on your selected category. General category cutoffs are the strictest. Typical relaxations are:

  • OBC-NCL: 5–8 percentile points lower than General for most B-schools and law colleges
  • SC: 15–25 percentile points lower than General
  • ST: 20–30 percentile points lower than General
  • EWS: Similar to OBC-NCL (varies by institution)
  • PwD: Additional relaxation of 5–10 points on top of category cutoff

How to Improve Your Chances

If your predicted results show mostly Reach colleges for your target, here are the realistic strategies:

  • Give more mock tests. Most students who improve significantly from one attempt to another do 30–50 mock tests. Identify weak topics, not just overall score.
  • Target exams with lower competition. XAT for XLRI, MH-CET for JBIMS, NMAT for NMIMS — these are separate exams from CAT with their own difficulty curves and candidate pools.
  • Use category advantage. If you're OBC/SC/ST, the predictor shows realistic cutoffs for your category — which may open significantly more colleges than the General cutoff suggests.
  • Consider ROI over ranking. FMS Delhi (₹2.3L fees, ₹29–34L avg placement) and JBIMS Mumbai (₹2.5L fees, ₹20L+ placement) offer better ROI than many higher-ranked private colleges that cost ₹25–35L.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which BBA college can I get with CUET 85 percentile?

With CUET 85 percentile General category: Gargi College DU (B.Com, 88 cutoff — slightly above but worth trying), DDU Delhi (BBA, 90 cutoff — reach), Jamia Millia Islamia (BBA, 80 cutoff — safe), Christ University Bangalore (BBA, 80 cutoff — safe), NMIMS Mumbai via NPAT (separate exam), and many other central universities. Use the predictor above for exact results by category.

What is the CAT percentile required for IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta?

All three IIMs (ABC) require 99+ percentile for General category shortlisting. However, the percentile is just the first filter — final selection also considers academic profile (10th, 12th, graduation marks), work experience (2–4 years preferred), gender diversity, and academic diversity (non-engineering graduates get preference). Many 99+ percentile candidates don't receive calls due to academic background. OBC cutoff is 95+, SC is 85+, ST is 80+.

Which MBA college has the best ROI in India?

FMS Delhi has the best MBA ROI in India — total fees ₹2.32 lakh for the full 2-year programme, with average placement of ₹29–34 LPA. Payback period is under 2 months of salary. JBIMS Mumbai is second-best ROI — ₹2.5L fees, ₹20L+ placement. Both require high percentiles (FMS: CAT 98+, JBIMS: MH-CET 99.9+ for Maharashtra domicile). Among IIMs, IIM Indore has the lowest fees at ₹18L with ₹25L average placement.

Which NLU should I target with CLAT rank 500?

With CLAT rank 500 General category: NLU Jodhpur (closing rank ~400 — reach), NUJS Kolkata (~300 — reach), NLU Bhopal (~700 — safe), NLU Patna (~800 — safe), NLU Raipur (~1000 — safe). Top 3 NLUs (NLSIU Bangalore, NLU Delhi, NALSAR Hyderabad) need ranks under 100–200. For OBC category, rank 500 opens NLU Jodhpur and NUJS Kolkata more comfortably. Use our CLAT predictor for category-specific results.

Can I apply to multiple exams for MBA admissions?

Yes — this is strongly recommended. Most top B-schools accept multiple exam scores. NMIMS Mumbai accepts both NMAT and CAT. XLRI accepts XAT (primary) and also considers GMAT. Many B-schools accept CAT, XAT, NMAT, CMAT, ATMA, and MAT. Appearing in CAT + XAT + NMAT significantly expands your college options and increases your chances of getting a good call even if one exam doesn't go as planned.

Is the college predictor accurate for 2026 admissions?

The predictor uses actual cutoff data from the 2025–26 admissions cycle. Cutoffs typically change by 1–3 percentile points each year based on exam difficulty and application volume. Treat the results as a realistic guide, not a guarantee. Always verify with official college websites before applying. For the most competitive colleges (99+ percentile), even small fluctuations can affect results — always apply to a mix of Safe, Moderate, and Reach options.
