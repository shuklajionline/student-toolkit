Frequently asked questions

Are Student Toolkit tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up or login. Calculators run in your browser, so your marks and attendance are not stored on our servers.

How do I calculate how many classes I can bunk?

Use the Bunk Meter. Enter classes attended and total classes held, choose your college’s minimum (usually 75%), and it shows how many classes you can skip safely or how many you must attend to recover.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Most Indian universities use CGPA × 9.5 for a 10-point scale, but some (such as VTU, Mumbai University and Anna University) use their own formulas. The CGPA Calculator supports these grading systems and converts automatically.

Which tool helps me choose a college?

The College Predictor takes your CUET, CAT, JEE, IPMAT, XAT, MH-CET, NMAT, CLAT or NIFT score and shows colleges you can get, with cutoffs, fees and placements. The College Directory has honest reviews of individual colleges.

How do I make a resume as a fresher in India?

Use a one-page, single-column format with clear headings: contact details, education with CGPA, skills, projects, internships and achievements. Write each point as action + what you did + result with a number. Our ATS resume guide for freshers has the full format, examples and a checklist.

Which tool should I use to plan studying abroad?

Use the Study Abroad Finder. It matches 28 destinations to your budget and course, shows total cost in rupees, visa and proof-of-funds rules, post-study work rights, scholarships and an education-loan EMI estimate.
