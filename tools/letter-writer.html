    How to write a formal application (Indian format)

    1. Address block

    Start with “To,” then the designation (“The Principal,”), institution and city, each on its own line. In Hindi, start with “सेवा में,”.

    2. Date & subject

    Write the date in full (7 November 2026). The subject should say exactly what you want: “Application for sick leave from 10 to 12 November 2026”.

    3. Salutation

    “Respected Sir/Madam,” for principals and HODs. “Dear Dr. Iyer,” for emails to professors. “Dear Hiring Team,” when you don’t know the recruiter’s name.

    4. Body

    Who you are (name, course, roll number) → the situation and dates → your request → what you’ll do (submit certificate, cover syllabus). Three short paragraphs is ideal.

    5. Closing

    “Thanking you,” then “Yours obediently,” (traditional, for college authorities) or “Yours sincerely,” (modern, for offices and companies). Sign with name, roll number, course and phone.

    6. Email etiquette

    • Specific subject line
    • Under 150–200 words
    • One clear request with a date
    • Send from a professional email ID
    • Follow up once after 5–7 days

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I write a leave application for college?

    Address it to the Principal or HOD with “To,” and the college name, add the date and a clear subject line (for example “Application for sick leave from 10 to 12 November”), greet with “Respected Sir/Madam,”, state who you are (name, course, roll number), the reason and exact dates, mention any medical certificate, request leave, and close with “Thanking you, Yours obediently,” followed by your name, roll number and course. The sick leave template above fills all of this for you.

    What is the format of a formal letter to a principal in India?

    To, → The Principal, → College name, → City. Then Date, Subject, salutation (Respected Sir/Madam), body paragraphs, “Thanking you,”, “Yours obediently,” (or “Yours sincerely,”), your name, roll number, course and phone, and Place.

    How should I write an email to a professor?

    Use a specific subject line, greet them by title and name (Dear Dr. Iyer), introduce yourself in one line with your course and roll number, make one clear request with dates, keep it under 150–200 words, and close politely with your full name and course.
