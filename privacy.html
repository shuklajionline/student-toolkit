Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

No accounts

You do not need an account to use Student Toolkit.

Data saved on your device

Some tools save your entries, such as attendance subjects, in your own browser. This stays on your device unless you clear it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited. Google may use cookies for this. See how Google uses this data.

Advertising

Some pages may load Google AdSense, which can use cookies to show and measure ads. You can manage personalised ads in Google's ad settings.

AI tools

When you use the resume checker, resume generator or Check My College, the text you submit is sent through our server to an AI provider, currently Groq, to create a response. We do not store it ourselves. Remove personal details such as your phone number or address from anything you do not want to share.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@studenttoolkit.online.
