About Student Toolkit

Student Toolkit is a free website for college students in India. It brings everyday student tools and college research into one place, with no sign-up.

What you can do here

  • Track attendance and see how many classes you can miss.
  • Calculate CGPA and convert it to percentage.
  • Work out in-hand salary from a CTC offer.
  • Shortlist colleges from your entrance exam score.
  • Compare career paths for your stream.
  • Read BBA and MBA college reviews and student guides.

How we work

Our editorial policy explains where our information comes from, how AI is used and how to report a mistake.

Independence

Student Toolkit is not affiliated with NIRF, UGC, AICTE or any college. Our tools give estimates, so check anything important with your college or the official source.
