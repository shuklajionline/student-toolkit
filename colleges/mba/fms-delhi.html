Overview & Reputation

Established in 1954, FMS Delhi is the oldest management school under Delhi University and one of India's oldest. The school sits on the North Campus of Delhi University in a modest but functional building. The campus itself is utilitarian — nothing like the large IIM campuses — but the Delhi location is its greatest asset.

FMS has approximately 220 students per batch, making it more intimate than the larger IIMs. The curriculum is rigorous, with strong emphasis on case-based learning. Delhi's corporate ecosystem — consulting, FMCG, banking, government-adjacent industries — gives FMS students recruiting access comparable to IIM ABC at a fraction of the cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is FMS Delhi the best MBA ROI in India?

₹2.32 lakh total fees ÷ ₹32 lakh average placement = 0.07 year payback. That means FMS pays for itself in under 1 month of salary. No other MBA in India — at any fee level — comes close to this ratio.

What CAT percentile is needed for FMS Delhi?

98 percentile overall with 85+ in each section. Lower than IIM ABC (which requires 99+), but FMS compensates with an extremely rigorous GD+PI process. Many 99.5 percentile CAT scorers fail the FMS interview.

FMS Delhi vs IIM Bangalore — which is better?

FMS Delhi: better ROI (₹2.3L fees vs ₹26.5L), slightly lower placements (₹32L vs ₹34.8L). IIM-B: better campus, stronger for tech/startups, triple accreditation. For Finance/Consulting: FMS has marginal edge on ROI. For Tech: IIM-B wins. If you're cost-conscious, FMS is unbeatable.

Is FMS Delhi a government college?

Yes — FMS is the Faculty of Management Studies under Delhi University, a central government university. This is why fees are so low (government-subsidised) while maintaining Tier 1 placement quality.
