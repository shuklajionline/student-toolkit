Overview & Reputation

IIM Mumbai was established in 1963 as the National Institute of Industrial Engineering (NITIE). In 2023, it was converted to an Indian Institute of Management, becoming the 21st IIM. It holds NIRF #6 in Management for 2025 — its highest rank ever, surpassing IIM Calcutta and IIM Indore.

The institute is located in Powai, Mumbai — one of India's most prime corporate locations. It has India's strongest alumni network in Operations, Supply Chain, and PSUs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, GAIL).

📊 Unique Selling Proposition:
• #1 in Operations/SCM — No other B-school in India comes close
• PSU Placements — IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, GAIL, ONGC recruit in large numbers
• Mumbai Location — Corporate access unmatched for supply chain roles
• Lowest Fees among Top IIMs — ₹17L vs IIM A/B/C ₹30-35L

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Mumbai good for MBA?

Yes — IIM Mumbai (formerly NITIE) is India's #1 for Operations & Supply Chain Management. NIRF #6 in 2025. PSUs like IOCL, BPCL, HPCL recruit heavily. Fees ₹17L, average placement ₹25L. ROI ~0.68 years — excellent.

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Mumbai?

General category typically needs 96+ percentile for a call. Operations background helps. Realistic safe score: 97+ percentile.

What is the fee for IIM Mumbai MBA?

Total fees for 2-year PGDIM at IIM Mumbai is approximately ₹17 lakh — one of the lowest among top IIMs.

Is IIM Mumbai better than IIM Lucknow for Operations?

Yes — For Operations and Supply Chain specifically, IIM Mumbai is significantly better than IIM Lucknow or any other IIM. For general management or marketing, IIM Lucknow is better. Choose based on your career goal.
