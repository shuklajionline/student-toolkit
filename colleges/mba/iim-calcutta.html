Overview & Reputation

IIM Calcutta was established in 1961 — the same year as IIM Ahmedabad — making it one of the two founding IIMs. Located at Joka on the outskirts of Kolkata, the campus spans 135 acres and is architecturally distinctive. The PGP batch size is approximately 480 students.

IIM-C has historically dominated finance and investment banking placements. The proximity to Kolkata — India's traditional finance capital before Mumbai — combined with the IIM-C alumni network's deep penetration of financial services, makes it the preferred destination for students targeting Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, and PE/VC firms. NIRF 2025 placed it at #7 — a slight drop from its historical #5 position.

Honest Pros & Cons

✓ Strengths
✓
India's #1 for Finance and Investment Banking — Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and major PE firms recruit at IIM-C in numbers that equal or exceed IIMA specifically for finance roles. If IB is your goal, this is the right school.
✓
One of India's founding two IIMs (est. 1961) — IIM-A and IIM-C were established in 1961. The heritage and alumni network depth that comes with 60+ years of producing India's top managers is unmatched.
✓
135-acre iconic Joka campus — Arguably India's most scenic IIM campus. The iconic library building and green spaces create a distinct academic environment.
✓
Strong alumni in global financial hubs — IIM-C alumni are in senior positions at Goldman, Citi, HSBC, and Morgan Stanley globally — not just in India. International finance career pathways are real.
✗ Weaknesses
✗
NIRF dropped to #7 in 2025 — From its historical #5 position, IIM-C slipped to #7. Worth monitoring — suggests some relative decline vs IIM Kozhikode and Lucknow.
✗
Kolkata location — Kolkata's economy has contracted relative to Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore. Internship access outside Finance is more limited than metro IIMs.
✗
High fees (₹33.5L) vs IIMB (₹26.5L) — Paying ₹7L more than IIMB for the same average package is hard to justify unless Finance is your specific goal.
✗
Less strong for Tech and Startups — For tech careers and startups, IIMB and IIMA are both superior. IIM-C's strength is concentrated in Finance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IIM Calcutta's average placement 2025?

₹34.23 LPA average, highest domestic ₹85+ LPA for IB/PE roles. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, KKR, and McKinsey are top recruiters. 100% placement rate consistently.

Is IIM Calcutta the best for Finance MBA in India?

Yes — for Investment Banking and Finance specifically, IIM-C is India's #1. Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan recruit in higher volumes at IIM-C than at any other Indian B-school.

IIM Calcutta vs IIM Bangalore — which to choose?

If Finance/IB is your goal, IIM-C. If Tech/Startups, IIM-B. IIM-B also has lower fees (₹26.5L vs ₹33.5L) and better location for non-Finance careers. For Consulting, both are equal.

What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Calcutta?

99 percentile overall plus 90+ in each section. Academic performance (Class 10, 12, graduation) also heavily weighted in IIM-C's composite shortlisting score.
