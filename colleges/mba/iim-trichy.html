Overview & Reputation

IIM Trichy was established in 2011 as part of the third generation of IIMs. Located in Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Tamil Nadu, the institute has steadily built its reputation. In NIRF 2025, it secured #14 ranking — ahead of IIM Ranchi (#16), IIM Nagpur (#19), and IIM Jammu (#21).

The institute is known for its strong focus on academic rigor, case-based learning, and growing industry connections. Its location in Tamil Nadu provides access to Chennai's corporate ecosystem (2 hours away), as well as Bangalore and Hyderabad.
