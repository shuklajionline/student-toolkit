Overview & Reputation

IIM Udaipur was established in 2011 as part of the third generation of IIMs. Despite being one of the newer IIMs, it has achieved remarkable growth. In NIRF 2025, it secured #12 ranking — surpassing IIM Nagpur (#19), IIM Jammu (#21), and even some older institutions.

The institute is located on a 300+ acre campus on the outskirts of Udaipur, known as the "City of Lakes." The campus is modern, fully residential, and considered one of the most beautiful B-school campuses in India.
