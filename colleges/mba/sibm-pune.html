Overview & Reputation

SIBM Pune was established in 1978 as part of the Symbiosis group. It is the flagship B-school of Symbiosis International University. The institute is located in Pune, Maharashtra — one of India's key corporate hubs with strong FMCG, IT, and manufacturing presence.

SIBM Pune holds NAAC A+ accreditation and is consistently ranked among India's top private B-schools. The institute is known for its strong Marketing curriculum, excellent FMCG placements, and balanced student life with multiple cultural and business festivals.

What is the SNAP cutoff for SIBM Pune?

General category typically needs 96+ percentile in SNAP. SNAP is less competitive than CAT — 96 percentile is achievable with focused preparation.

What is the fee for SIBM Pune MBA?

Total fees for 2-year MBA at SIBM Pune is approximately ₹22 lakh, including tuition, accommodation, and other charges.

Is SIBM Pune worth the fee?
