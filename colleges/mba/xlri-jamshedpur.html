Overview & Reputation

XLRI Jamshedpur was founded in 1949 by Jesuit missionaries, making it India's oldest private B-school — predating the IIMs by 12 years. The school offers two flagship 2-year programmes: HRM (Human Resource Management) and BM (Business Management). Both are highly regarded, but HRM is uniquely dominant — XLRI HRM alumni hold more CHRO and HR Director positions at India's top companies than alumni of any other institution.

XLRI uses XAT (Xavier Aptitude Test), its own entrance exam, rather than CAT. XAT is conducted in January and is widely considered harder than CAT for Verbal Reasoning. GMAT is accepted for international applicants.

Honest Pros & Cons

✓ Strengths
✓
India's #1 HR MBA — no substitute — If HR leadership (CHRO, HR Director, People & Culture) is your goal, XLRI HRM is non-negotiable. The alumni network in HR is deeper than any IIM for HR-specific roles.
✓
Oldest private B-school in India (est. 1949) — The institutional culture, Jesuit academic rigor, and alumni relationships built over 75 years cannot be replicated by newer schools. Alumni loyalty is exceptionally strong.
✓
Two distinct flagship programmes — BM and HRM serve completely different career goals. Students choose their programme at admission — allowing for more focused career development than IIM's generalist approach.
✓
XAT vs CAT — different applicant pool — XAT is conducted separately, often by candidates who can't crack CAT. This means XLRI accepts some excellent students who might not have cleared IIM cutoffs — creating a diverse batch.
✓
Strong in Jamshedpur = focus on studies — Unlike Bangalore or Delhi, Jamshedpur's limited city life paradoxically improves academic focus. Students report deeper engagement with coursework.
✗ Weaknesses
✗
Jamshedpur location is a real weakness — Jamshedpur is an industrial city with limited corporate access. Internships, case competitions, and networking events are harder to attend than from Delhi, Mumbai, or Bangalore.
✗
XAT is a different exam requiring separate prep — CAT and XAT prep overlap significantly but XAT has a unique 'Decision Making' section that CAT doesn't. Students must allocate separate prep time.
✗
BM programme has smaller batch than top IIMs — The XLRI BM batch (~120 students) is small, which means fewer recruiting companies per student — but placement quality remains Tier 1.
✗
NIRF #10 — lower than IIM ABC — While placement quality is Tier 1, NIRF methodology weights research output heavily. XLRI is less research-intensive than IIMs, which affects its NIRF rank.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is XLRI the best HR MBA in India?

Yes, unambiguously. XLRI HRM alumni hold more CHRO and HR Director positions at Fortune 500 companies and India's top conglomerates than alumni of any other Indian B-school. For HR leadership careers, no other institution comes close.

What is the XAT cutoff for XLRI?

95 percentile in XAT overall, with section-wise cutoffs in Verbal and Analytical Reasoning. XLRI also conducts a Written Ability Test + Personal Interview for final selection.

XLRI BM vs IIM Lucknow — which is better?

For HR: XLRI wins clearly. For FMCG/Marketing: IIM Lucknow is stronger. For Consulting: both are similar. Placement averages are comparable (XLRI BM ₹31L vs IIM-L ₹28L). Choose based on career goal.

Does XLRI accept CAT scores?

No — XLRI uses XAT as its primary entrance exam. GMAT scores are accepted for international applicants and some lateral-entry programmes.
