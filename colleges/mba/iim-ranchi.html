Overview & Reputation

IIM Ranchi was established in 2010 as the 8th Indian Institute of Management. It was mentored by IIM Calcutta (one of the older IIMs) in its early years, giving it strong academic foundations. In NIRF 2025, it secured #16 ranking — ahead of IIM Nagpur (#19), IIM Jammu (#21), and IIM Bodh Gaya (#25).

The institute is known for its HR specialization (PGDM-HRM), which is one of only a few in India alongside XLRI. The main campus is in Ranchi, with plans for a larger permanent campus in the coming years.
