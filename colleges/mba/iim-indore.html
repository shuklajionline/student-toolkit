The IPM alone makes IIM Indore uniquely valuable — no other route gives you an IIM degree starting from Class 12 with comparable brand value.

Overview & Reputation

Established in 1996, IIM Indore is one of the second-generation IIMs that has consistently improved. Its 450-acre campus in Rau-Pithampur, on the outskirts of Indore, is one of India's largest B-school campuses. The PGP is a standard 2-year programme, but IIM Indore's IPM (Integrated Programme in Management) — a 5-year BBA+MBA hybrid for Class 12 students — is its most distinctive offering.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IIM Indore's IPM programme?

IPM (Integrated Programme in Management) is a 5-year programme starting after Class 12. Years 1–3 cover foundation and management courses (equivalent to BBA), and Years 4–5 are the MBA equivalent (PGP). Graduates receive an IIM Indore degree with full IIM brand credibility. Admission is through IPMAT, IIM Indore's own entrance exam.

IIM Indore IPM vs IIM Kozhikode IPM — which is better?

IIM Indore's IPM is older (since 2011) and has a larger alumni network. IIM Kozhikode's IPM is newer (2024) with no placement track record yet. For 2026 applicants, IIM Indore IPM is clearly the safer, more proven choice.

What is the IPMAT cutoff for IIM Indore?

IPMAT cutoff varies but typically requires a score in the top 5% of applicants across Quantitative Aptitude and Verbal Ability sections. Class 12 score (minimum 60% for general) is also required.
