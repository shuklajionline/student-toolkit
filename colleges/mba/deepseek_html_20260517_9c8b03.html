Overview & Reputation

TAPMI (TA Pai Management Institute) was established in 1980 in Manipal, Karnataka. It is one of India's older private B-schools. The institute holds AACSB accreditation — a globally recognized accreditation held by only ~5% of B-schools worldwide, including ISB, IIM Calcutta, IIM Bangalore.

TAPMI is known for its strong finance curriculum and banking placements. The Manipal campus is beautiful and conducive to focused studying, though the location is not a corporate hub.
