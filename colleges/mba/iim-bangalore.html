If consulting is your goal, both are equal. The Bangalore location — India's Silicon Valley — is a structural advantage no other IIM has.

Overview & Reputation

Established in 1973, IIM Bangalore sits on a 100-acre campus in south Bangalore. The PGP (2-year MBA equivalent) has a batch size of ~450 students. IIMB's academic culture is rigorous — the case-method pedagogy, borrowed from Harvard and Stanford traditions, demands heavy preparation. The Entrepreneurship Cell (e-Cell) is one of India's most active, and IIMB alumni dominate the Indian startup ecosystem.

IIMB is triple-accredited (AACSB, EQUIS, AMBA) — one of fewer than 100 institutions globally to hold all three. In MBAUniverse 2026 rankings, IIMB is #2. In FT Global MBA rankings, IIMB's executive programmes consistently rank in the top 50–75 globally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IIM Bangalore's average placement in 2025?

IIM Bangalore's average placement in 2025 is ₹34.88 LPA. The highest domestic package exceeded ₹80 LPA (tech and PE roles). Top recruiters include McKinsey, BCG, Amazon, Google, Goldman Sachs, and Sequoia Capital. Placement rate is 100%.

What are IIM Bangalore's total fees?

Total fees for the 2-year PGP at IIM Bangalore are approximately ₹26.5 lakh — about ₹8.5 lakh less than IIM Ahmedabad for near-identical placement outcomes. Education loans without collateral are available.

IIM Bangalore vs IIM Ahmedabad — which is better?

Both are India's top 2 and the choice depends on career. IIM-A is marginally better for consulting (MBB) and traditional finance. IIM-B is stronger for tech, entrepreneurship, and global startups. IIM-B also has lower fees and better location for tech careers.

What CAT percentile is needed for IIM Bangalore?

99 percentile overall plus 90+ in each section. IIMB also heavily weights academic performance (Class 10, 12, graduation) and work experience in its composite shortlisting score.
