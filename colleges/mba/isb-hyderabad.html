Bottom Line First: ISB is India's only globally-ranked MBA (FT Top 50) and is the fastest route to top consulting and PE roles for professionals with 2+ years experience. The 1-year format means lower opportunity cost. However at ₹40L+ fees for a 1-year programme, the per-year cost is the highest of any Indian MBA. Only justified if you have work experience and need a global-recognizable credential.

Overview & Reputation

Founded in 2001 with partnerships from Kellogg, Wharton, and London Business School, ISB was designed from the ground up as an internationally competitive 1-year MBA. The Hyderabad campus spans 260 acres and is one of India's most modern B-school campuses. A second campus in Mohali (Punjab) was added in 2012.

ISB holds triple accreditation — AACSB, EQUIS, and AMBA — making it one of fewer than 100 institutions globally with all three. It consistently ranks in the FT Global MBA Top 50 (typically #40–55). The ISB PGP (Post Graduate Programme) requires a minimum of 2 years work experience and targets mid-career professionals wanting a fast-track to senior roles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ISB Hyderabad better than IIM Ahmedabad?

For freshers: IIM-A is better. For professionals with 3–5 years experience: ISB often delivers better ROI due to the 1-year format and global brand. For international careers specifically, ISB's FT ranking gives it an edge. Both are India's top programmes — the right choice depends on your profile and goals.

What GMAT score is needed for ISB?

ISB's median GMAT score is approximately 720. A 700+ score is typically needed for admission. For consulting-targeted applicants, 730+ is recommended. GRE and CAT are also accepted.

Is ISB a 1-year or 2-year MBA?

ISB's PGP (Post Graduate Programme) is a 1-year full-time programme requiring 2+ years work experience. It also offers PGPMAX (2-year part-time for senior executives) and other executive programmes.

What is the total fees at ISB Hyderabad?

Total fees are approximately ₹40+ lakh for the 1-year PGP programme, including tuition, hostel, and other charges. This is India's highest MBA fee in absolute terms — though the 1-year format reduces total opportunity cost vs 2-year IIM programmes.
