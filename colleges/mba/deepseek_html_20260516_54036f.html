Overview & Reputation

Great Lakes Institute of Management was founded in 2004 by Dr. Bala V. Balachandran (former professor at Kellogg School of Management). The Chennai campus is the flagship campus. The institute holds AMBA accreditation (one of the triple crown accreditations).

Great Lakes is best known for its 1-year PGDM program designed for experienced professionals (2+ years work experience). It also offers a 2-year PGDM for freshers. The institute has a strong focus on Analytics and Data Science, with dedicated programs and strong placement outcomes in these fields.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Great Lakes Chennai good for MBA?

Yes — Great Lakes Chennai is known for its 1-year PGDM for experienced professionals. Average placement ₹16 LPA, fees ₹18 lakh. Strong in analytics and data science. Good ROI for experienced candidates.

Great Lakes vs IMT Ghaziabad — which is better?

IMT has higher placements (₹18L vs ₹16L) but both have similar ROI (1.06 vs 1.13 years). IMT is better for Marketing. Great Lakes is better for Analytics and 1-year PGDM for experienced candidates.

What is the GMAT/CAT cutoff for Great Lakes?

For 1-year PGDM: GMAT 600+ or CAT 85+ percentile. For 2-year PGDM: CAT 80-85+ percentile. Work experience is strongly preferred.

What is the fee for Great Lakes Chennai MBA?

Total fees for 1-year PGDM is approximately ₹18 lakh. 2-year PGDM is approximately ₹20 lakh.

Is Great Lakes good for freshers?

No — the 2-year PGDM for freshers has poor ROI (₹20L fees, ₹14L placement). Freshers should target IMT, NMIMS, or TAPMI instead. Great Lakes is only recommended for experienced professionals joining the 1-year PGDM.
