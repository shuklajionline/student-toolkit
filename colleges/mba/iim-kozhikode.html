Bottom Line First: IIM Kozhikode hit NIRF #3 in 2025 — its highest ever, leapfrogging IIT Delhi DMS and IIM Lucknow. For students scoring CAT 95–97, IIM-K is the most underrated Tier 1 IIM. Lower fees than IIM-B or IIM-C, strong general management placements, and arguably India's most scenic B-school campus in the Kerala hills. Genuinely undervalued by applicants who fixate on IIM ABC.

Overview & Reputation

Established in 1996 and set on a stunning hilltop campus in Kozhikode, Kerala, IIM Kozhikode has rapidly climbed the rankings — reaching NIRF #3 in 2025. The campus is widely regarded as India's most beautiful IIM setting. The school offers a PGP (2-year MBA equivalent) with a batch of ~400 students and is particularly strong in general management, marketing, and digital business.

Honest Pros & Cons

✓
Most beautiful IIM campus in India — Kozhikode hilltop campus is genuinely breathtaking. The academic environment — surrounded by Kerala greenery — is unlike any other IIM. Many alumni cite campus life as a career highlight.
✓
Lower fees than old IIMs (₹21L) — ₹21L vs ₹26.5L (IIMB) or ₹33.5L (IIM-C) for a NIRF #3 school. The value proposition is exceptional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did IIM Kozhikode rank NIRF #3 in 2025?

IIM-K's NIRF score improved significantly in Teaching & Learning Resources and Research output, placing it ahead of IIT Delhi DMS and IIM Lucknow. It reflects genuine institutional improvement over 5 years of investment in faculty quality and research infrastructure.

IIM Kozhikode vs IIM Lucknow — which to choose?

NIRF: IIM-K is #3, IIM-L is #5. Fees: IIM-K ₹21L vs IIM-L ₹20L — similar. Placements: IIM-L ₹28L avg vs IIM-K ₹26L. For FMCG/Marketing: IIM-L wins. For general management and CAT students in the 95–97 range: both are excellent choices.

What CAT score is needed for IIM Kozhikode?

96 percentile overall with 85+ in each section. IIM-K's composite score also weighs academics and work experience, similar to other IIMs.
