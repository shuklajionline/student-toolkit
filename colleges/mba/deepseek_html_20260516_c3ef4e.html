Overview & Reputation

IIT Bombay's Shailesh J. Mehta School of Management (SJMSOM) was established in 1995. It has consistently ranked among India's top B-schools, leveraging the prestigious IIT Bombay brand. Located in the financial capital of India, SJMSOM offers unparalleled access to corporate internships and networking opportunities.

The school is known for its rigorous academic environment, strong industry connections, and a curriculum that blends technology and management — a unique value proposition in Indian B-school landscape.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIT Bombay MBA good?

Yes — IIT Bombay SJMSOM is a premier IIT MBA. Average placement ₹28 LPA, fees ₹12 lakh. ROI ~0.43 years — excellent. Best for finance, operations, and product management roles. Mumbai location is a major advantage.

What is the CAT cutoff for IIT Bombay MBA?

General category typically needs 97+ percentile. Engineering background strongly preferred. Work experience is valued.

What is the fee for IIT Bombay MBA?

Total fees for 2-year MBA at IIT Bombay SJMSOM is approximately ₹12 lakh — one of the lowest among top B-schools.

IIT Bombay MBA vs IIM Mumbai — which is better?

Both are excellent. IIM Mumbai is #1 for Operations. IIT Bombay SJMSOM is better for Finance and Product Management. Choose based on your career goal. Both have excellent ROI.

Can non-engineers get into IIT Bombay MBA?

Yes, but it's very difficult. IIT Bombay SJMSOM strongly prefers engineering graduates. Non-engineers need exceptional CAT scores (99.5+) and outstanding profiles.
