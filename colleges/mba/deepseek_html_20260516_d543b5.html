Overview & Reputation

IMT Ghaziabad was established in 1980 as one of India's first private B-schools. It is the flagship institute of the IMT Group. The institute holds AACSB accreditation — a globally recognized accreditation shared by only a handful of Indian B-schools including ISB, IIM Calcutta, IIM Bangalore.

Located in Ghaziabad (Delhi NCR), IMT has strong corporate connections with companies in Gurgaon and Noida. The institute is known for its strong Marketing curriculum, excellent sales placements, and vibrant campus life.
