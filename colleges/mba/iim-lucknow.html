For FMCG and Marketing careers specifically, IIM-L has historically been India's strongest placement ecosystem. The ₹20L fee structure makes it the best ROI among the old IIMs outside FMS.

Overview & Reputation

Established in 1984, IIM Lucknow is the fourth IIM in India after Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta. Located on a sprawling green campus in Lucknow's Prabandh Nagar, IIML has a batch of approximately 460 students. The campus is considered one of India's most beautiful IIM campuses, set on 200 acres with extensive greenery.

IIML climbed to NIRF #5 in 2025 (up from #7 in 2024) — reflecting genuine improvement in research output and placement quality. The school is particularly renowned for its marketing and FMCG placements — HUL, P&G, ITC, Nestle all recruit in significant numbers at IIM-L.

Honest Pros & Cons

✓ Strengths
✓
NIRF #5 with lower CAT cutoff (97 vs 99+) — The combination of NIRF #5 ranking with a 97 percentile cutoff (vs 99+ for IIM ABC) means IIM-L is the best 'reachable' old IIM for students in the 96–98 range.
✓
India's #1 for FMCG and Marketing careers — If HUL, P&G, ITC, Nestle, or Asian Paints is your target, IIM-L has historically placed more students at these firms than any other IIM. The school's brand in FMCG HR is unmatched.
✓
Beautiful 200-acre campus in Lucknow — IIM-L's campus is considered one of India's most scenic. The academic environment and campus life quality are genuinely excellent.
✓
Lowest fees among older IIMs (excl. FMS) — ₹20L vs ₹26.5L (IIMB) or ₹33.5L (IIM-C) — for near-comparable placement quality, IIM-L's fee advantage is real and meaningful.
✓
Improved NIRF ranking (+2 in 2025) — Climbing from #7 to #5 signals genuine institutional improvement in research and outcomes — a positive trend worth noting.
✗ Weaknesses
✗
Lucknow location limits some recruiting access — While Lucknow is a large city, it doesn't match Delhi, Mumbai, or Bangalore for corporate recruiting density. Some consulting and tech firms don't conduct campus visits.
✗
Average placement (₹28L) below IIM ABC — The gap between ₹28L and ₹34–35L is real — students who can get into IIM ABC should not choose IIM-L over them.
✗
Less strong for Finance vs IIM-C — IIM-C's Finance placement track record is distinctly better. Finance-focused students should target IIM-C if possible.
✗
Noida satellite campus — Some students are placed at IIM-L's Noida campus rather than Lucknow. Verify which campus you'd join during admissions — facilities differ.

Frequently Asked Questions

IIM Lucknow vs IIM Kozhikode — which is better?

IIM Lucknow: stronger for FMCG/Marketing, NIRF #5. IIM Kozhikode: NIRF #3, stronger academic research reputation. For careers, IIM-L's industry connects are wider. For academics, IIM-K is stronger. Both are genuine Tier 1.

What CAT percentile for IIM Lucknow?

97 percentile overall plus 85+ in each section. Lower than IIM ABC (99+) but still elite — only top 3% of CAT takers qualify.

Is IIM Lucknow good for FMCG careers?

Excellent — IIM-L is arguably India's #1 for FMCG marketing placements. HUL, P&G, ITC, Nestle, Asian Paints, Marico all recruit in significant numbers at IIM-L every year.

What is IIM Lucknow's NIRF ranking?

NIRF 2025: #5 Management — up 2 positions from #7 in 2024. This reflects improving research output and student outcomes.
