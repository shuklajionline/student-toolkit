How admission works

Fees: what to expect

Two-year MBA/PGP total fees range roughly from ₹12 lakh at newer IIMs to ₹25 lakh+ at the oldest IIMs. Most IIMs offer need-based financial aid.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on iimrohtak.ac.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for Indian Institute of Management (IIM)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Strong brand with recruiters
  • Rigorous curriculum and peer group
  • Financial aid available

Things to check

  • Very high CAT percentiles needed at older IIMs
  • Newer IIMs’ placements and campuses vary
  • High fees relative to some government B-schools

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
