Before you take admission

An AISHE listing means the institution reports data to the Ministry of Education — it is not, by itself, approval of every course. Check the course on its regulator’s list (AICTE, NMC, BCI, PCI, INC, NCTE, COA) and the university on UGC’s lists. Use our 21-point college check and the College Predictor.
