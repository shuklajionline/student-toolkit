Fees: what to expect

B.Tech tuition at NITs is about ₹1.25 lakh a year (plus hostel and mess), with fee waivers for SC/ST/PwD students and income-based remission similar to IITs.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on nitmz.ac.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for National Institute of Technology (NIT)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Government-funded, affordable fees
  • Home-state quota helps local students
  • Strong placements at older NITs (Trichy, Surathkal, Warangal, Calicut, Rourkela and others)
  • Diverse student body from across India

Things to check

  • Outcomes vary widely between older and newer NITs
  • Branch matters a lot for placements
  • Infrastructure at some newer NITs is still developing

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
