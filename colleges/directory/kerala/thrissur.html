Before you take admission

Being listed in AISHE means an institution reports data to the Ministry of Education — it is not, by itself, an approval of every course. Check the course on its regulator’s list (AICTE, NMC, BCI, PCI, INC, NCTE, COA) and the university on UGC’s lists.
