FAQs

What is the Grand College Directory?

A free, searchable directory of Indian colleges and universities — currently 326+ institutions including all IITs, NITs, IIMs, operational AIIMS, NLUs and central universities, plus leading state, private and autonomous colleges. Each profile shows the type of institution, recognition, entrance exams, courses and how to verify fees and placements.

Will every college in India be listed?

Yes. The directory lists all 70,542 institutions in the official AISHE directory of the Ministry of Education — 1,362 universities, 52,509 colleges and 16,671 standalone institutions — searchable by name, state and district. Detailed profiles are being added in batches, starting with the most-searched institutions.

Where does the data come from?

Institution facts (location, type, year of establishment, recognition) come from official and public sources such as the institutions’ own websites and government lists. Fees, cutoffs and placements change every year, so profiles point you to the official fee page and the institution’s NIRF data instead of printing numbers that go out of date.

Are these profiles reviews?

Profiles give type-level strengths and weaknesses (for example, what is common to IITs or NLUs). In-depth, college-specific reviews are marked with a “Full review” link. Profiles are reviewed by our editors before they are shown in search engines.

How do I check if a college is genuine?

Check the university on UGC’s lists, the course on its regulator’s approved list (AICTE, NMC, BCI and others), and the NAAC grade on the NAAC website. Our 21-point checklist walks you through it.
