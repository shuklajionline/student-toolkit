Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on snu.edu.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for Deemed-to-be university (private)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Modern infrastructure and flexible curricula at good institutions
  • Many offer strong industry links

Things to check

  • High fees
  • Quality and placements vary widely — check NIRF data and verified placement reports
  • Beware “management quota” and agent fees

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
