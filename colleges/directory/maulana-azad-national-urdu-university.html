Fees: what to expect

Central universities charge low fees for most undergraduate and postgraduate programmes; professional courses cost more.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on manuu.edu.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for Central Universitys — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Low fees and government recognition
  • Students from across India
  • Wide range of subjects

Things to check

  • Placements depend heavily on the course and department
  • Some newer central universities have limited infrastructure

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
