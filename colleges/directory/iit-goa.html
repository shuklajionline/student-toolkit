Fees: what to expect

B.Tech tuition at IITs is about ₹2 lakh a year (plus hostel and mess). Under Ministry of Education rules, SC/ST/PwD students and students with family income below ₹1 lakh get a full tuition waiver, and those with family income between ₹1–5 lakh get a two-thirds waiver.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on iitgoa.ac.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for Indian Institute of Technology (IIT)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Strong national brand and alumni network
  • Research facilities and faculty quality among the best in India
  • Fee waivers make it affordable for lower-income families
  • Good placement and higher-studies outcomes in most popular branches

Things to check

  • Extremely competitive entry through JEE Advanced
  • Newer IITs (set up after 2008) have smaller alumni networks and placements that vary a lot by branch
  • Heavy academic workload and relative grading
  • Some campuses are far from big cities

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
