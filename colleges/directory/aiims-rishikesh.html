Fees: what to expect

MBBS fees at AIIMS institutes are very low — typically a few thousand rupees a year — plus hostel charges.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice on aiimsrishikesh.edu.in, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • Among the lowest-cost, highest-quality medical education in India
  • Huge patient load and clinical exposure (especially older AIIMS)
  • Strong research culture

Things to check

  • Extremely high NEET-UG ranks needed
  • Newer AIIMS institutes are still building hospitals and faculty strength

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
