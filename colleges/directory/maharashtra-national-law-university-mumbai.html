How admission works

5-year integrated LLB and LLM: CLAT, with seats allotted through the Consortium of NLUs counselling. Many NLUs have a state domicile quota.

Fees: what to expect

Five-year law programmes at NLUs typically cost about ₹2–3.5 lakh a year including hostel; check the official fee page for exact figures.

Fees change every year. Use the official fee notice, and compare the total cost with the median salary in the institution’s NIRF data. Education loan? Read our loan guide and convert salaries with the salary calculator.

Strengths and things to check

General points for National Law University (NLU)s — not a college-specific review.

Common strengths

  • BCI-approved, well-regarded law degrees
  • Strong moot court and placement culture at top NLUs
  • Residential campuses

Things to check

  • Fees are high compared with other government universities
  • Outcomes vary a lot between older and newer NLUs

Verify before you apply

  1. Confirm the institution and your course on the right regulator’s list (UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, COA, PCI) for this year.
  2. Read the institution’s latest NIRF data file for median salary and number of students placed.
  3. Check the NAAC grade and its validity on the NAAC website (if applicable).
  4. Talk to current students and check where recent graduates work on LinkedIn.
