Overview & Reputation

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya College (DDU) is a constituent college of Delhi University, established in 1990. It offers BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) as one of its flagship programmes. The college is affiliated with Delhi University and its degrees carry the DU brand. Admission is through CUET (Common University Entrance Test), with cutoffs slightly below SSCBS.

Honest Pros & Cons

✓ Strengths
✓
₹1.77L fees — extraordinary value — Second only to SSCBS (₹72K) and Loyola Chennai (₹70K) in terms of fee-to-placement ratio for quality BBA colleges. DU government subsidisation keeps costs minimal.
✓
Lower CUET cutoff than SSCBS — SSCBS requires 95+ CUET and Maths. DDU requires ~88–93 CUET without mandatory Maths requirement. Makes it accessible to strong students who don't have Maths background.
✓
Full Delhi NCR corporate access — Same Delhi advantage as SSCBS — consulting, banking, FMCG, government all accessible for internships and placements from Delhi location.
✓
DU brand widely recognised across India — A degree from Delhi University carries recognition in corporate India that private BBA colleges at 5–10x the fee can't always match.
✗ Weaknesses
✗
Less well-known than SSCBS for corporate BBA recruiting — SSCBS is a dedicated business school. DDU is a general college with BBA as one of several programmes. Corporate HR teams that specifically seek SSCBS graduates may not be as familiar with DDU.
✗
Many students go for further studies — Like all DU BBA colleges, a significant proportion of students pursue CA, MBA (CAT), or postgrad — the placement rate for those going directly into corporate roles is lower than SSCBS.
✗
Maths not required — reduces Finance competitiveness — SSCBS's Maths requirement creates a more Finance-capable cohort. DDU without Maths has a more mixed skill base — slightly less competitive for quantitative Finance roles.

Frequently Asked Questions

DDU Delhi BBA vs SSCBS Delhi — which is better?

SSCBS is better for dedicated BBA quality, Finance focus, and stronger corporate brand. DDU is better if you don't have Maths in Class 12 or scored 88–93 in CUET (below SSCBS 95+ cutoff). At essentially the same fees, DDU is the only comparable alternative to SSCBS in Delhi.

What is the CUET cutoff for DDU Delhi BBA?

~88–93 percentile in CUET, varying by year and reservation category. Significantly lower than SSCBS (95+) and does not require Maths as a mandatory subject.

Is DDU Delhi a government college?

Yes — DDU is a constituent college of Delhi University, fully government-funded. This explains the extremely low fee of ₹1.77L for 3 years.
