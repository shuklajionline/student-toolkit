Overview & Reputation

Established in 1987 under Delhi University, SSCBS (Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies) was created specifically to offer a professional undergraduate management education. Unlike general arts or commerce colleges, SSCBS is entirely focused on business — all faculty, curriculum, and recruiting infrastructure is designed for management careers.

The college offers BBA (Financial Investment Analysis), BBA (Computer Applications), and B.Com (Hons) programmes. The flagship is BBA (FIA) — highly regarded for Finance, Consulting, and corporate careers. Admission is through CUET (Common University Entrance Test), requiring 95+ percentile and Maths as a compulsory Class 12 subject.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is SSCBS Delhi the best BBA in India?

₹72,000 total fees for 3 years. ₹7–9 LPA average placement. Payback under 2 months. Strong DU brand. Delhi location for internships. Best alumni pathway to IIM ABC and FMS. No private BBA at any price point delivers better value.

What is the CUET cutoff for SSCBS?

95+ percentile in CUET (UG) with Maths as a compulsory Class 12 subject. Students without Maths in Class 12 are ineligible for BBA (FIA) — the flagship programme.

SSCBS vs NMIMS BBA — which is better?

SSCBS is better in ROI — ₹72K vs ₹12L. NMIMS is better in campus experience, Mumbai location, and private college infrastructure. If ROI is your priority (it should be for BBA), SSCBS wins decisively. If campus life and Mumbai placement access matter, NMIMS is justified.

Does SSCBS have a hostel?

No — SSCBS does not have residential hostels. Students arrange private accommodation in Delhi. This adds ₹5,000–15,000/month to the effective cost, though it's still far cheaper than private BBA colleges.
