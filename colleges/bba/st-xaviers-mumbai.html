Bottom Line First: St. Xavier's College Mumbai offers one of India's best undergraduate management ROIs — ₹2.3L total fees for a 155-year-old institution with NAAC A accreditation and Mumbai's entire corporate ecosystem as its placement backdrop. The BMS programme is widely respected by Mumbai employers. For Maharashtra students who can't get into JBIMS, Xavier's BMS is the highest-quality alternative at the lowest cost.

Overview & Reputation

St. Xavier's College was established in 1869 by Belgian Jesuit missionaries — making it one of India's oldest and most prestigious degree colleges. The autonomous college is affiliated with the University of Mumbai and offers BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies) — the Mumbai University equivalent of BBA.

The college holds NAAC A (the second-highest NAAC grade) and is consistently ranked among India's top 100 colleges. The Churchgate/CST campus is centrally located in South Mumbai — minutes from the Nariman Point business district, HDFC, Goldman Sachs, and Deloitte offices. The BMS programme has approximately 120 students per batch.

✗ Weaknesses
✗

✗

✗
Placement outcomes below NMIMS — Average placement (₹5.5L) is lower than NMIMS (₹6.8L) despite similar or better brand. Mumbai University curriculum limitations partly explain this.
✗
Maharashtra domicile preference — Like many Mumbai University colleges, Xavier's admissions through CET can favour Maharashtra domicile students for management-quota seats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is St. Xavier's Mumbai good for BMS/management studies?

Yes — 155-year legacy, NAAC A, Mumbai location, ₹2.3L fees. Xavier's BMS is one of India's best value management degrees. ROI payback under 6 months. Widely respected by Mumbai Finance, Consulting, and FMCG employers.

St. Xavier's BMS vs NMIMS BBA — which is better?

Xavier's: better ROI (₹2.3L vs ₹12L fees, similar placement outcomes for non-Finance roles). NMIMS: better for Finance specifically, NMAT/MBA pipeline, modern campus. If cost matters: Xavier's is clearly superior. If Finance/NMIMS MBA pipeline is the goal: NMIMS.

What is the admission process for BMS at St. Xavier's Mumbai?

Admission is through the Maharashtra DTE/Mumbai University CET process. Xavier's is autonomous — it may conduct its own additional selection process. Class 12 marks and CET score determine admission.
