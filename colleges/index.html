Why Student Toolkit

Different from Shiksha.
Different from everyone.

Every other college directory in India takes money from colleges to boost their rankings. We don't. Here's exactly what makes us different.

01
Zero Sponsored Rankings
Shiksha, Collegedunia, and Careers360 charge colleges to appear higher in rankings and bury negative reviews. We have never taken money from any college. Not one rupee. Our rankings are purely based on NIRF 2025, verified placement data, and real student outcomes.
02
We Expose Bad Colleges
Other directories don't expose bad colleges because those colleges pay them. We do. We have a dedicated "Worst Colleges" section with specific red flags for each bad actor — fake placements, lapsed accreditation, management quota scams, aggressive cold-call admissions. Named and explained.
03
Real Verified Data Only
Every fee figure, placement number, and NIRF rank comes from NIRF 2025, MBAUniverse 2026, official placement reports, and cross-verified LinkedIn alumni data. We never use college-supplied marketing brochures as our source.
04
ROI Calculator Approach
We publish the ROI payback formula (Total Fees ÷ Annual Placement = Payback in years) for every college. A college that charges ₹20L and places at ₹8L has a 2.5-year payback — we call that out honestly. No other directory does this.
05
Not Just Rankings — Verdicts
Every college page has an honest verdict: Must Apply, Worth It, Use Carefully, or Avoid. We tell you which colleges are stepping stones, which are traps, and which are genuinely excellent for specific career paths. Judgment, not just data.
06
100% Free Forever
All college profiles, rankings, comparison tables, and guides are completely free. No premium tier, no subscription, no registration. We are funded by the belief that students deserve honest information, not by colleges paying us to mislead them.
