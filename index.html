How to Maintain 75% Attendance in College

Maintaining 75% attendance is mandatory in many Indian colleges. If your attendance drops below this limit, you may face detention, exam restrictions, or internal mark penalties.

To calculate your attendance percentage: Attendance % = (Lectures Attended ÷ Total Lectures) × 100

If you want to know how many lectures you can safely bunk, use our Attendance Calculator above.

Strategy tips: • Avoid continuous bunk streaks • Track attendance weekly • Keep buffer above 80% • Attend important credit-heavy subjects
