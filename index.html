FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students ask about Student Toolkit.

What is Student Toolkit and what tools does it offer?

Student Toolkit is a free website offering online tools for college and university students. Current tools include a free attendance calculator, CGPA calculator, AI-powered resume checker, and a professional letter & email writer. All tools are completely free, require no sign-up, and work instantly in your browser.

How does the free attendance calculator work?

Enter your total number of classes and the number you have attended. The calculator instantly shows your current attendance percentage, how many more classes you can safely skip while staying above the minimum, or how many classes you need to attend to reach the required percentage.

Is the CGPA calculator free and accurate?

Yes — the CGPA calculator is 100% free, requires no sign-up, and works for all university grading systems. Enter your subject grades and credit hours and it calculates your semester CGPA instantly. It supports custom grade scales used by different institutions.

How does the AI resume checker help students?

The AI resume checker analyzes your resume against ATS (Applicant Tracking System) standards used by real employers. It gives you a score out of 100 and detailed, recruiter-style feedback on what to improve — keyword gaps, formatting issues, weak bullet points, and missing sections — so you can fix your resume before applying for internships or jobs.

Can I generate a leave application or professor email for free?

Yes. The free Letter & Email Writer generates professional leave applications, professor emails, internship request letters, recommendation request letters, scholarship inquiry letters, and academic grievance letters. Enter your details, pick a tone, and your letter is ready in seconds — completely free, no sign-up needed.

Are all Student Toolkit tools really free forever?

Yes. All tools on Student Toolkit are completely free with no hidden paywalls, no premium tiers, no subscription fees, and no sign-up required. We are committed to keeping every tool free for students permanently.
