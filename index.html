FAQ

Everything students ask us

What is Student Toolkit and what tools does it offer?

Student Toolkit is India's free platform for college students — attendance calculator, CGPA calculator, AI resume checker, letter writer, AI career path finder, and India's most complete BBA & MBA college rankings. All tools are 100% free with no sign-up required.

How does the free attendance calculator work?

Enter total classes held and classes attended. Instantly see your attendance percentage, how many classes you can safely skip while staying above 75%, or how many you need to attend to reach the minimum requirement.

How does the AI resume checker help students?

The AI resume checker analyzes your resume against ATS (Applicant Tracking System) standards, gives a score out of 100, and provides recruiter-style feedback on keyword gaps, formatting issues, and weak bullet points — so you can fix it before applying for internships or jobs.

Are all Student Toolkit tools really free forever?

Yes. All tools are permanently free — no paywalls, no premium tiers, no subscription fees, no sign-up required. Ever.

Which is India's #1 MBA college in 2026?

IIM Ahmedabad — NIRF 2025 #1, MBAUniverse 2026 #1, average placement ₹35+ LPA. FMS Delhi is the best ROI: just ₹2.32 lakh total fees with ₹29–34 LPA placements, matching IIM ABC outcomes.

Which BBA college has the best ROI in India?

SSCBS Delhi — total fees ₹72,000 for 3 years, average placement ₹7–9 LPA. Payback period under 2 months. No private college at any fee level comes close to this value. Requires Maths in Class 12 and 95+ percentile in CUET.

What are the NIRF 2025 top 10 management colleges?

NIRF 2025 Management Top 10: 1. IIM Ahmedabad, 2. IIM Bangalore, 3. IIM Kozhikode, 4. IIT Delhi DMS, 5. IIM Lucknow, 6. IIM Mumbai, 7. IIM Calcutta, 8. IIM Indore, 9. MDI Gurgaon, 10. XLRI Jamshedpur.

How do I identify a fake or bad MBA college in India?

Check AICTE approval at aicte-india.org, NAAC grade at naac.gov.in, NIRF rank at nirfindia.org. Search 50+ alumni at claimed top-recruiters on LinkedIn. If the college cold-called you with an admission offer — that alone is a red flag. Good colleges do not chase students.
