MBA Admission Timeline 2026

MBA admissions in India follow a structured annual cycle. Entrance exams such as CAT, XAT, NMAT and SNAP determine admission into top management institutes. Understanding the MBA admission timeline helps aspirants prepare strategically.

June – August

CAT and other MBA entrance exam notifications are released. Registration begins during this period.

September – November

Application deadlines close. Students focus on exam preparation and mock tests.

November – December

Major MBA entrance exams such as CAT are conducted.

January – March

Results are declared. Shortlisted candidates attend GD-PI rounds.

April – June

Final admission offers are released. Academic sessions begin around July.
