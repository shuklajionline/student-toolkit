Editorial Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Where our information comes from

College pages draw on NIRF, college websites, published placement reports and other public sources. Reported figures can differ between sources and change every year, so always confirm fees, cutoffs and placements with the college before you apply.

What our verdicts are

Tiers, pros and cons, and verdicts are our opinions based on public information. They are not official rankings.

How AI is used

We use AI tools to help draft and structure some pages. The resume and college tools also generate AI responses on request, and these can be wrong, so check them before relying on them.

Pages under review

Profiles in the College Directory are hidden from search engines until an editor has checked them. Our in-depth BBA and MBA reviews are public, but fees, placements and cutoffs change every year, so confirm the latest numbers with the college before you apply.

Ads

Some pages may show advertising or promote related services. Ads do not decide what we write about a college.

Corrections

If something is wrong, tell us and include a source if you can. We review reports and update the page when a correction is needed.
